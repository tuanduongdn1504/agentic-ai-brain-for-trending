04 · Honest caveats

Trust, but verify

Authenticity is not vendor-guaranteed. These are extracted prompts; the only validation shown is a screenshot of "Claude confirming a prompt is authentic" — a model confirming its own leaked prompt is weak evidence. Assume staleness (snapshots drift the moment a vendor ships). Extraction method undisclosed + crowd-sourced ("PRs Welcome") → per-file provenance varies. Gray-zone genre — fine to read/learn; don't build a product on it or copy vendor prompt text verbatim. It's a passive archive: nothing to install, no capability added.
