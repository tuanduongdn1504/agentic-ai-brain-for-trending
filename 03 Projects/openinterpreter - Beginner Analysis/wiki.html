02Harness emulation — the one wiki-worthy idea

A model's agentic performance isn't just the model — it's the harness it was RL-tuned against: the exact system prompt + tool schemas + turn loop + formatting. Kimi K3 was tuned against Moonshot's "Kimi Code" harness; run it under a foreign harness and you leave capability on the table.

Open Interpreter ships one binary that becomes any harness on demand. Point it at a cheap/open model and select that model's native harness → "maximum K3 performance with a Codex-like interface." The Kimi-Code harness was reimplemented Rust-native for exactly this. Swappable via /harness:

native claude-code claude-code-bare kimi-code kimi-cli qwen-code deepseek-tui ↝ v72 zcode ↝ GLM v176 swe-agent minimal

Two of the emulated harnesses are corpus subjects — DeepSeek-TUI (v72) and the Z.AI/GLM harness (GLM-5, v176) — making this quietly corpus-recursive. (The DeepSeek-TUI lineage is a hand-caveated cross-ref, not a source-verified #57.)

Why it matters for the vault (Goal #1): reading OI's claude-code vs kimi-code harness definitions is a rare, concrete way to see what makes Claude Code effective, by contrast — the productized form of the "the harness is the lever, not just the model" thesis (loop-engineering v189 / grok-build v215 / the harness-engineering thread).
