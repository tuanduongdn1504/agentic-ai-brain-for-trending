The 10 rules (the core artifact)

Output "shaped for ADHD cognition, not merely brief" — rests on 5 neurocognitive facts (small working memory, understanding↔doing friction, startup difficulty, uniform time perception, dopamine scarcity). Loosely adapted from Ramsay & Rostain's Adult ADHD Tool Kit.

  1. Lead with action — first line is doable now; command/path before prose.
  2. Number multi-step tasks — each step one bounded action; cut trivial steps.
  3. End with a concrete next action — ONE thing doable in under two minutes.
  4. Suppress tangents — finish the first issue; offer others separately.
  5. Restate state every turn — reader can't hold multi-step context between messages.
  6. Specific time estimates — "15 minutes" beats "some work."
  7. Make wins visible — show what works concretely.
  8. Matter-of-fact on errors — state cause and fix directly.
  9. Cap lists at 5 — split into "now" vs "later."
  10. No preamble, recap, or pleasantries.

Plus explicit override conditions (explain requests, destructive actions, debug spirals, ambiguity). Trigger /i-have-adhd; always-on via touch ~/.claude/.i-have-adhd-always (a SessionStart hook).
