The rule

A check cannot survive someone with standing to overrule it

The gap here is not between checked and unchecked. It is between the surfaces a maintainer reads and the surface a buyer reads — and the difference is not care. A maintainer is afraid of getting the code wrong. Nobody is afraid of getting the pitch wrong.

It fired, it was right, and overruling it was free.
