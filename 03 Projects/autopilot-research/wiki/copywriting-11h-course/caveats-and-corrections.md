# Caveats & Corrections

**Everything that would mislead a reader who trusted the raw video or the raw agent extraction: one false statistic, four misleading numbers, two extraction distortions caught in verification, a wall of unverifiable income testimonials, and heavy ASR garble on the single most important word in the course.**

## Source

[Khoá Học Copywriting 11 Tiếng Cho Người Mới](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zuIudgESKDk) · Trương Phương · 2026-07-07 · 11:20:22 · Vietnamese (auto-captions, rough ASR). Full verdicts in [[claims-scorecard]].

## 1. The one FALSE claim

- **"The brain processes images 60,000× faster than text" (C4).** The course teaches this verbatim (*"bộ não con người xử lý hình ảnh nhanh hơn 60.000 lần so với văn bản"*) to justify visual storytelling. It is a **debunked marketing myth** with no scientific basis — traced to a 1982 *Business Week* article; no behavioural study supports a 60,000× multiplier (real estimates range 6×–600× and are context-dependent). The article [[storytelling-golden-structure-6-point]] flags it in place; the *emphasis on narrative* stands on other grounds, the number does not.

## 2. Misleading numbers (real-sounding, materially wrong)

- **Email ROI "40:1" (C1)** — an attribution figure from DMA/Litmus surveys, widely cited. Independent holdout-test research puts *incremental* email ROI ~3× lower (≈12:1). The course uses the inflated figure to sell email copywriting as the road to $1,000/month. Handled in [[email-marketing-owned-asset-vs-ads]].
- **"Dan Henry pioneered the online-course industry / made $40M from social media" (C6)** — doubly wrong. First, this framing was **NOT in the transcript** (the course only mentions Dan Henry as a story about choosing the right tool) — it was an over-reach in the automated extraction, and is **not asserted anywhere in this wiki**. Second, externally it is misleading: Dan Henry entered an already-established market, and his ~$40M came from coaching/webinars, not social media.
- **"700–1,000 cold emails over 3–4 months for the first client" (C11)** — course pessimism used to argue you need structured training. In reality, *volume without personalisation* fails; personalised outreach + follow-up compresses the timeline toward ~1 month. See [[customer-acquisition-niche-selection-lead-magnets-outreach]].
- **"~80% of replies come from follow-up emails" (C24)** — the instructor conflated two different stats: "80% of *sales* need 5+ follow-up touches" vs. "% of *replies* from follow-ups" (the latter ≈ 42–55%). Follow-ups matter; the attribution is overstated ~1.5–2×.

## 3. Extraction distortions caught during verification (Rule 12 — fail loud)

These are errors the *analysis pipeline* introduced, not the course — caught by refute-first verifiers and corrected before shipping:

- **The "Zara" millionaire story (C14).** An early digest attributed a $3K→$20K→millionaire trajectory to a person named "Zara." Verification found the trajectory is told **first-person — it is the instructor's (Trương Phương's) own narrative**, not a "Zara" case study. No copywriter named "Zara" with these claims exists externally. The wiki does **not** present a Zara case study; the only mention is this correction and a note in [[critical-appraisal]].
- **The Dan Henry over-reach (C6, above).** Same class of error — the extraction inflated a passing mention into an authority claim. Removed.

## 4. Unverifiable income testimonials (course marketing, treat as aspirational)

Every named income claim appears **only in the course's own video** — no portfolio, Upwork profile, LinkedIn, or third-party record corroborates any of them:

- Students: **Chi Tiên** 31M VND/mo (2 mo), **Chi Trâm** 48M VND/mo (3 mo), **Quỳnh Như** $400/mo (2 mo).
- Cited experts: **"Claway"** 350K subs from 77 videos (no such channel found), **Laura Hitchins** $30–50K/mo on 12 hrs/week (no public record).
- Instructor **(C7)**: 5M VND was an *aspirational goal* (stated as a wish), not earned income; 20M/mo is corroborated in course materials; **75M/mo is unverified**.

Several testimonials (31M, 48M VND ≈ $1,200–1,900) **exceed the course's own "$1,000/month" title** — a standard enrolment-marketing pattern. Full context in [[critical-appraisal]].

## 5. ASR / transcription garble

Auto-captions on 11h20m of Vietnamese produced **141 logged garbles**. The most consequential: **the word "copywriting/copywriter" is mangled throughout** — heard as *copying, C reading, copator, copaider, C writing, card reading, CRER, briider*. Every analysis inferred the correct term. Other representative garbles:

Because the ASR is this rough, **specific numbers, names, and quotes were verified against the transcript before use** rather than trusted from a single caption line.

## Key Takeaways

- The course's **craft is sound; its evidence is marketing.** Trust the frameworks and process; discount every income number.
- **One outright-false statistic** (60,000×) and **four misleading benchmarks** — none load-bearing for the actual skills.
- The pipeline's own errors (Zara, Dan Henry) were caught by adversarial verification and corrected — the value of refute-first checking.
- If you re-watch the video, the single hardest thing to trust is any **named income figure**; the single hardest thing to *hear* is the word "copywriting" itself.
