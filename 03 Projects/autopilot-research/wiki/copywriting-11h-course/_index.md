## The one framing

**Copywriting is the skill of designing psychologically-grounded, research-heavy messages that persuade a specific customer to act** — learned through deliberate practice, monetised by acquiring clients via warm/cold outreach, and scaled toward $1,000+/month by building an owned email list and repeatable templates. The course teaches this well; it *sells* it with income numbers that mostly don't survive verification (see [[claims-scorecard]] · [[caveats-and-corrections]]).
