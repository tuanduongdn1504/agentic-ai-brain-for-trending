## The three that check out (CONFIRMED)

- **C5 — Franklin's method.** The study-a-master → note the ideas → rewrite from notes → compare → fix weaknesses loop is genuinely Benjamin Franklin's self-teaching technique from his autobiography. Accurately taught. Anchors the [[deliberate-practice-common-mistakes-mental-resilience]] article.
- **C15 — 30.7M e-commerce sites (2024).** Widely-corroborated market figure (±methodology). Used to size the opportunity in [[what-is-copywriting-core-definition]].
- **C16 — Vietnam #10 for AI-slop views (1.69B).** Matches the 2026 Kapwing "AI Slop Report". Used to argue human copy beats AI spam.

## The numbers that don't (headline corrections)

- **C4 (FALSE)** — the "60,000× faster" statistic is a debunked internet myth (1982 *Business Week*), not neuroscience.
- **C1 (MISLEADING)** — 40:1 email ROI is an attribution figure; independent holdout tests show incremental return ~3× lower (~12:1).
- **C24 (MISLEADING)** — the instructor conflated "80% of sales need 5+ follow-ups" with "80% of replies come from follow-ups" (real ≈ 42–55%).
- **C8/C9/C10/C19/C20 (UNVERIFIABLE)** — every named income testimonial appears only in the course's own materials; zero external corroboration.
