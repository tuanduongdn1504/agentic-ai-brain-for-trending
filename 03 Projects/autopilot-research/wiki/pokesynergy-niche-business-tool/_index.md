## The one-paragraph version

Scoriox — a solo Australian creator who **vibe-codes tools for gaming communities** — built PokéSynergy, a free browser app that collapses the painful, multi-tool, manual chore of competitive-Pokémon team optimization (spreadsheet + separate damage calculators + tribal meta knowledge) into **one explainable, edit-check-edit workspace**, and distributes it via a **YouTube-demo → app → Discord** funnel. It is real, live, free, and built on Next.js. The transferable lesson for the operator's Goal #2 is the *shape*: pick a painful recurring optimization in a passionate niche, make it click-to-tune with live "here's why" feedback, encode expert defaults, and distribute through content. The honest caveats are equally valuable: the video **oversells** a "perfect spreads in seconds" auto-optimizer that the live product doesn't actually claim; the business rides on **Nintendo/TPCi IP at their forbearance**; the niche is **small** (~10–15K active players); and Scoriox can build slowly only because a **YouTube audience subsidizes** it — i.e. this is inspiration for *UX/workflow patterns*, not a business-model template to copy wholesale.
