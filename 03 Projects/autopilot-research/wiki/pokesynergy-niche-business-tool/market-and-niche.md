# Market & Niche

*The tool's demand base. Verified via Workflow `dive:market` + main-loop WebSearch. The market claims hold up.*

## Pokémon Champions is a real game

- **The Pokémon Company** title, launched **Nintendo Switch / Switch 2, 2026-04-08**. The **official software for the 2026 Pokémon World Championships** (Moscone Center, San Francisco, Aug 28–30, 2026; ~$160K prize pool) and qualifying Championship Series events. Play! Pokémon competitions officially **transitioned to Champions** in April–May 2026.
- **Ranked ladder** is live, with persistent tiers (Poké Ball → Great Ball → Ultra Ball → Master Ball → Champion). Singles and Doubles are **separate ladders**. Every player resets to Poké Ball Rank 4 each season (no carryover).

## "Season 4" checks out (correcting an earlier ingest doubt)

The video's *"season 1 … heading into season 4"* is **correct**. Ranked **Season M-4** runs **July 7 – Aug 4, 2026**, entry window **July 16 – Aug 4** — i.e. the season opened the exact week of the video's July 16 upload. (An earlier ingest note wondered if this conflicted with the "M-A/M-B" labels — it doesn't: **M-A/M-B are *regulation sets*** governing which Pokémon are legal, while **M-1…M-4 are the *ranked seasons***. Both true, different axes.)

## VGC Doubles & the "Big Six"

- **VGC (Video Game Championships) Doubles** = the sanctioned competitive format: bring 6, battle 4 (2v2), regulation-locked team building. Real and correctly described.
- ⚠️ **"Big Six" is a caveat.** It's *legacy* VGC16 terminology (Primal Groudon / Xerneas / Mega-Kangaskhan / Talonflame / Mega-Salamence / Smeargle). In the **current** Champions meta the term isn't the active label; the meta is described by **individual usage tiers** (Garchomp ~46%, Archaludon ~45%, Meowscarada ~43%) and **archetypes** (Tailwind Offense, Standard Goodstuffs, Sun+Trick Room). Scoriox is likely using "Big Six" colloquially for a current top-6 archetype or as nostalgic shorthand. Concept valid; naming loose.

## Niche size — focused but real

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Active online ranked players (est.) | **~10,000–15,000** globally (from ~178K competitive battles logged May 21–Jun 17, 2026 ÷ ~15–20 battles/player) |
| Regional event attendance | 291–1,013 players/event; 14+ regionals across NA/EU/SA/Oceania |
| Esports viewership (peak) | NA International Champs ~75K peak; Wolfe Glick Invitational ~20.5K peak |
| Prize pools (2026 YTD) | ~$193K cumulative; Worlds $160K |
| Community infra | Pokémon Showdown, LimitlessVGC, ChampsDex, ChampionsLab, MunchStats + Discords |

**Interpretation:** a **focused, measurable, tool-comfortable niche** — small (thousands, not millions) but engaged, with money and infrastructure around it. Big enough to serve; too small to be a large standalone business (see [[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/business-model-and-risks]]).

## Relevance to the operator

- This niche is **currently growing** (game launched April 2026) — good timing for a tool, which partly explains PokéSynergy's traction.
- The audience is **already comfortable with specialized tools + Discord + YouTube tutorials** — low education cost for a new tool. Recruitment audiences (recruiters, hiring managers) are analogously tool-comfortable but a **vastly larger TAM** (see [[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/critical-appraisal]] on TAM inversion).

## Key Takeaways

- Pokémon Champions, its ranked ladder, **Season M-4**, and VGC Doubles are all **real and correctly described** — the video's market claims hold.
- "**Big Six**" is legacy/colloquial, not a current formal archetype — minor caveat.
- The niche is **~10–15K active players**: real demand, small ceiling.
