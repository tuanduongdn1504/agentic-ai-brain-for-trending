## Part 1 — Scoriox's actual funnel (measured, not inferred)

### It's a *pivot*, and a repeatable creator pattern

Scoriox's channel is **8,460 subscribers / 561 videos** — and it is **not primarily a Pokémon channel.** The back catalog is **crypto/Web3 gaming** (dozens of Illuvium videos — "I'm Betting Everything on Illuvium", staking breakdowns — plus Origins TCG, Playa3ull, Pirate Nation, Tokyo Beast). He **pivoted to Pokémon Champions in 2026**, when the game launched.

Crucially, **he built community tools for his *previous* niche too**: his catalog includes an "Illuvium Revdis Calculator Tutorial", an "Ultimate Illuvium Team Builder Guide", and "Optimize Your Team with My Exclusive Spreadsheet." So PokéSynergy is **not a one-off** — it's an instance of a repeatable operator pattern: *cover a game as a creator → build the optimization tool that game's players need → funnel your audience to it.* This **strengthens Move 3/Move 5** of the playbook and adds a caution: the pattern is **creator-first**, i.e. the tool rides an audience the founder already had.

> ⚠️ **Overturn:** [[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/caveats-and-corrections]] had flagged the "Illuvium crossover" as low-confidence / *"possibly a name-collision, do not assert."* His own channel has **dozens** of Illuvium videos — the connection is **real and central to who he is**. Corrected to high-confidence.

Three things the data shows that inference couldn't:

1. **The winning hook is *transformation*, not *feature*.** "Changes How New Players **Build**" (empowerment) crushed "Solves Stat Spreads" (feature) and "The ONLY App" (superlative). The breakout sold *who you become*, not *what it does*.
2. **He A/B-tests hooks.** The same core line shipped as "This **Tool** Solves…" (Jul) and "This **App** Solves…" (May); "Best Tool" vs "ONLY App" vs "Saves Beginners Hours" are systematic beginner-hook variants.
3. **Reach ≫ subscriber count.** 8,460 subs but individual videos hit 19K and 45K — the funnel pulls in *non-subscribers* via algorithmic discovery, then the app + Discord convert them.

### Correction to the "336 views" caveat

[[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/critical-appraisal]] says "336 views is not a market signal." That's **literally true of the subject video** (it was ~1 day old) but **understates the campaign**: ~106K cumulative views across a sustained weekly funnel, with a 45K breakout. The honest framing: *don't read a market into any single video; do read a real, working, multi-month content funnel into the campaign.* Still measure the right things (below).

## Part 2 — Porting the funnel to a B2B recruitment tool (hireui)

PokéSynergy's loop is **YouTube demo → free app → Discord (QA + feature-request engine) → next video shows the new feature.** The recruiter-market analogue is **structurally different** because it's B2B: the **buyer ≠ the user** (a manager/finance approves; a recruiter uses), the sales cycle is longer, and **peer reviews + compliance proof outweigh vendor marketing**.

Where recruiters actually discover tools and consume content (researched, ranked by confidence):

| Channel | How recruiters use it | Format that works | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (primary) | tool discovery, founder-credibility check, peer recs | **founder personal posts (8× company-page engagement)**; carousels (~6.6%), native docs/case-studies (~5.85%), founder video demos (~5.6%); **weekly cadence ≈ 2× engagement** | High |
| **Recruiting Brainfood** (Hung Lee, weekly Sunday newsletter + community) | trend/tool curation; peer sharing | pain-point-specific how-tos, curated resources | High |
| **G2 / Capterra / TrustRadius** | formal evaluation + comparison | verified testimonials, ROI metrics, **SOC2/GDPR proof**, balanced reviews | High |
| **"Best ATS 2026" listicles** (People Managing People, Built In, Recruiterflow) | shortlist 3–5 tools; SEO entry | comparison frameworks, category picks, pricing transparency | High |
| **ROI calculators** (interactive lead magnets) | budget justification to finance | 3–5-question calculators returning a personalized ROI + PDF email-capture | High |
| **Recruiting Slack/Discord** (Hacking HR, PeoplePeople, HRtoHR) | peer Q&A ("what ATS for X?"); honest reviews | practitioner (not vendor) demos, templates, problem→solution threads | Medium |
| **Recruiting podcasts** (Talk Talent to Me, Recruiting Future, Offer Accepted) | thought-leadership, tool-eval framing | founder interviews on hiring problems + solutions | Medium |
| **YouTube** (SocialTalent 23K, SmartRecruiters) | product walkthrough before trialing | "build a pipeline in 10 min" workflow demos; "why we built this" | Medium |
| **Reddit r/recruiting** | candid peer feedback | ask-the-community threads; vendor participation risky | Low |

### The funnel analogue

**LinkedIn founder demo/case-study (the "video") → free trial on *sample/synthetic* data (the "free app", per [[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/hireui-translation]] A3) → G2 reviews + recruiting-community word-of-mouth (the "Discord") → an ROI calculator as the interactive lead magnet** (the closest B2B cousin to PokéSynergy's free tool — an interactive artifact that delivers value before the sale). The community-as-QA loop maps to a **design-partner / private Slack** of early recruiter customers, not a public Discord.

### Honest caveats (carry from [[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/critical-appraisal]])

- **It requires a builder-in-public temperament** (LinkedIn founder posts, weekly cadence). Not every founder wants to be the face — and in B2B, founder credibility is doing the work Scoriox's channel does.
- **Measure the right things:** Discord/community size, trials, activation, retention, review volume — **never video/post views alone** (the "336 views" trap).
- **B2B trust gates are real:** SOC2/GDPR and balanced third-party reviews matter more than a slick demo. A recruiting tool touching candidate data must lead with compliance (ties to the [[external|hireui candidate-LLM legibility ADR]] and [[api-security-7-techniques/_index]]).
- **Survivorship still applies:** Scoriox's funnel works partly because he *already had* a gaming audience to pivot. A cold-start recruitment tool has no pre-built audience — the LinkedIn/newsletter/community route is the substitute, and it's slower.

## Key Takeaways

- **PokéSynergy's distribution is a *pivot* by a creator-who-builds-tools** (crypto→Pokémon; he'd built Illuvium tools before) — the pattern is real but **creator-audience-first**.
- **The winning hook was transformation ("Changes how you build"), not feature ("solves spreads")** — and he **A/B-tests titles**; ~weekly cadence, ~106K cumulative, one 45K breakout.
- **"336 views" was the wrong denominator** — the *campaign* is the signal, not the newest video (correction to [[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/critical-appraisal]]).
- **The B2B port swaps channels and inverts trust:** LinkedIn-founder-content → free-trial-on-sample-data → G2/community proof → ROI-calculator lead magnet; buyer≠user, compliance-gated, no free virality.
