# Business Model & Risks

*The part the video doesn't cover — and the part the operator most needs before treating this as a template. Synthesized from Workflow `verify:novelty-monetization`, `verify:ip-risk`, and `critic:completeness`.*

## Monetization: none, deferred

- PokéSynergy earns **$0**: no paywall, ads, Patreon, Ko-fi, donations, or premium tier (verified). Terms defer it: *"Before a broad public launch or paid plans, these Terms should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel."*
- **Realistic paths** for a tool like this (none deployed): freemium feature-lock (batch export, API, advanced analysis) · creator support (Patreon/Ko-fi) · sponsorship (creators, TOs, brands) · white-label to communities/TOs · affiliate (brand-risky). Niche tools *can* convert well (3–5% at ~$5–15/mo) **because searchers have high intent** — but only if premium delivers real value.
- **Today PokéSynergy is an audience/credibility play, not a revenue business** — a loss-leader for Scoriox's YouTube channel and expertise brand.

## Risk 1 — IP precarity (CRITICAL, structural)

The tool uses **Nintendo / The Pokémon Company (TPCi) IP** (names, base stats, move data, and — via Showdown/Serebii — sprites). There is **no fan-tool licensing program**. Documented enforcement history:

- Pokémon Uranium (fan game) — shut down 2016
- Pokémon Prism (ROM hack) — C&D 2020 after 8 years
- Relic Castle (fan-game hub) — DMCA takedown 2024 (recent → TPCi is *actively* enforcing)

Incumbents survive by **forbearance, not permission**: avoid distributing ROMs/official sprites, position as educational/non-commercial, disclaim affiliation. **The cliff:** scaling to large visibility or monetizing raises the odds of a C&D. Survival-by-obscurity is **not a durable business moat**.

## Risk 2 — Platform-lifecycle dependency

Value is tied to TPCi keeping the game + meta alive:

- **Regulation sets change** (~quarterly: M-A → M-B) → old spreads/data go stale, requiring constant re-verification.
- **Ranked seasons reset** every ~4 weeks.
- **Generation lifecycle** ~3–4 years; a new generation would obsolete the damage data + meta.

The tool is **not evergreen** — it lives inside a platform's content cycle it doesn't control.

## Risk 3 — TAM ceiling

~10–15K active players × ~3–5% conversion × ~$10/mo ≈ **~$18K–135K/yr gross** before hosting + the founder's time. Viable as a **subsidized side project**; **not** a salary-replacing standalone business.

## Risk 4 — Survivorship / selection bias

Scoriox can afford to **vibe-code slowly and defer monetization only because his YouTube channel subsidizes it.** You're seeing **N=1 success** with **no visibility into how many solo builders tried and quit**. "Solo founder iterates a free tool into a business" is not a proven repeatable pattern from this single case.

## The contrast that matters for hireui

| Dimension | PokéSynergy | hireui |
|---|---|---|
| **Data/IP** | Nintendo's, lent at forbearance | **Recruitment data is yours** — infinite runway |
| **Platform** | Champions meta cycle (~3–4 yr) | **Companies hire forever** — evergreen |
| **TAM** | ~10–15K players | **Millions** of recruiters/hiring managers |
| **Monetization** | Deferred, obscurity-constrained | Direct B2B willingness-to-pay |
| **Moat** | UX + explainability (algorithm is commodity) | UX + explainability + **your own hire corpus** |

**The lesson isn't "copy the model" — it's "steal the UX/distribution patterns, and note that your recruitment tool is structurally *stronger* on every business axis PokéSynergy is weak on."**

## Key Takeaways

- **$0 revenue, deferred monetization** — an audience/credibility play, not a business (yet).
- **IP + platform + TAM + survivorship** are four structural fragilities; the first two are *existential and unavoidable* when you build on someone else's IP and live game.
- hireui inverts all four weaknesses — **own the data, evergreen market, huge TAM, direct monetization** — which is exactly why the operator should borrow the *patterns*, not the *business model*.
