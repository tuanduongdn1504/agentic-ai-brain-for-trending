# Overview — PokéSynergy & the video

## The subject

**PokéSynergy** ([pokesynergy.app](https://pokesynergy.app)) is a free, browser-based **team builder + damage calculator** for competitive **Pokémon Champions** (The Pokémon Company's competitive-battle game, VGC 2026 Doubles format). It is built and run solo by **Scoriox**, a competitive-Pokémon YouTuber who describes himself as vibe-coding useful tools for gaming communities.

## What the video is

[This Tool Solves Pokémon Stat Spreads In Seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vlh3n3WwQZI) (`Vlh3n3WwQZI`, 2026-07-16, 11:14, ~336 views, category *Gaming*) is an **11-minute screen-share demo** of PokéSynergy. Scoriox loads a competitive team ("the Big Six") and walks through building/refining EV **stat spreads** for each Pokémon:

- For each mon he picks moves / item / nature, then uses the tool's **Speed / Offense / Defense** views.
- He clicks specific **threats** ("survive a Wave Crash", "live a Flash Cannon", "outspeed Meowscarada", "live two Psychics") and the tool **shifts stat points** to hit that breakpoint, recomputing damage live.
- He **locks** stats (e.g. Speed) so later changes don't disturb them, toggles a **threat filter** and a meta-threat count, and uses an **auto-build** button that fills moves/ability/item/stats "from the top spreads people are using."
- He is candid about being early/solo: mentions **bugs to fix** and **features coming** (a nature-boosted Sp.Atk breakpoint display, a rain toggle for Wave Crash).
- Closing pitch: *"make incredibly perfect stat spreads … in a matter of seconds"* for *"any team you find online."* Ends asking for subs ("97% … not subscribed").

## Why this is in the corpus (the operator's framing)

The operator (building **hireui**, a recruitment SaaS — Goal #2) submitted this deliberately, saying: *"I want to inspire from it and make business tools like this."* So the topic is cataloged as a **niche-business-tool archetype** — a worked example of a solo builder turning a painful, repetitive, manual optimization task in a passionate niche into a shippable tool, and distributing it through content. See [[pokesynergy-niche-business-tool/the-niche-tool-playbook]].

This makes it the corpus' **first product-as-business-archetype topic** and **first competitive-gaming subject** — off the usual coding-tools theme, but squarely on the operator's "build software with these methods" goal. Closest siblings: [[jasonlee-claude-mobile-app/_index]] (an indie app build, but studied for *the Claude toolchain*) and [[mosh-ai-powered-apps/_index]] (a first LLM feature). Neither treats the *product itself* as the object of study.

## Key Takeaways

- PokéSynergy is a **real, live, free** competitive-Pokémon team-tuning workspace — verified by direct fetch, not just the video.
- The video is a **content-led distribution artifact**: demonstrate the pain, show the fix, funnel to the app + Discord.
