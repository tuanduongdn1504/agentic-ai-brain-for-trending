## What's verified

- **British**: HouseFresh Limited is UK-registered; Danny Ashton is based in Greater Manchester ✅
- **Hands-on testing**: buys air purifiers with own money, tests efficiency + running cost, calls out scam products ✅
- **The fall**: from ~**4,000** daily Google visitors to ~**200** — the **91%** figure is HouseFresh's own published number (May 2024) ✅
- **Ranking nuance** (refuter catch): HouseFresh's own article says it ranked **#2** — not #1 — for "best budget air purifiers" (held since May 2023). The video's "ranked near the top" (EN) is fine; any "#1" retelling is wrong

## The timeline the video compresses (important)

| Date | Event |

**"After a Google update" (singular) is the video's one real compression here** — it was a two-update, 8-month arc.

## ⚠️ Recovery: conflicting accounts (surfaced per Rule 7, not averaged)

- One verified thread (PPC.Land, Oct 2025): visibility recovered **above** pre-HCU levels after the June 2025 core update.
- A second agent's 2026 summary claimed traffic remained ~1,300/day vs the 4,000 baseline in late 2025 (i.e., partial).
- These are reconcilable (visibility index ≠ absolute traffic; recovery can be real in rankings and partial in visits) but **the corpus does not have a verified July-2026 traffic number for HouseFresh**. Treat "HouseFresh recovered" and "HouseFresh never recovered" as both unproven; "HouseFresh partially recovered and diversified" is the defensible summary.

## Why this case matters beyond the anecdote

- It is the **named, self-documented, primary-sourced** example — most "Google killed my site" stories are anonymous or unverifiable; HouseFresh published its own analytics.
- It demonstrates the **survival play**: original testing (defensible content) + channel diversification (YouTube, newsletter/beehiiv, community, a Linus Tech Tips collaboration) — rankings were not defensible, the *brand* was.
- It is the cleanest "what's your Google Zero?" retro artifact for any team whose growth depends on one distribution channel ([[../google-zero-open-web/_index|pilot method D1]]).

## Key Takeaways

- Verified end-to-end: British, tested-with-own-money, #2 ranking, 4,000→200 daily, 91% self-published figure.
- The damage was cumulative across the Sept 2023 HCU + March 2024 core update — the video's single-update framing is the compression to avoid repeating.
- Partial recovery began with the June 2025 core update; the exact 2026 state is unresolved — do not cite either extreme.
- The strategic lesson is channel-portfolio, not SEO tactics: HouseFresh's recovery levers were all *non-Google* surfaces.
