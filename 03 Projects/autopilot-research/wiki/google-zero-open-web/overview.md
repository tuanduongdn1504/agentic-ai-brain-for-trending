## The argument (8 chapters)

1. **The ghost town** — Google no longer sends people to websites; 58.5% of US searches end in zero clicks ([[zero-click-data]]); "Google Zero" (Nilay Patel); HouseFresh lost ~91% of its Google traffic ([[housefresh-case-study]])
2. **Code Red** — ChatGPT (Nov 2022) hit the click-selling toll-booth model; NYT-reported internal "code red"; Page/Brin recalled; innovator's dilemma: *to beat a chatbot, Google had to become one*
3. **The window shopper** — SGE (2023) → AI Overviews (May 2024); glue-on-pizza / eat-a-rock; the Pew numbers on click starvation ([[pew-ai-overviews-study]], [[ai-overviews-timeline]])
4. **The great purge** — updates pushed forum threads above expert sites ([[forum-boost-and-reddit-enclosure]])
5. **The $60M enclosure** — the Google-Reddit data deal and the robots.txt lockdown ([[forum-boost-and-reddit-enclosure]])
6. **The $2B extinction event** — Raptive's publisher-loss model; Germany's measured losses ([[publisher-economics]])
7. **The innovator's murder** — the OpenAI/Microsoft/Perplexity race; the Aug 2024 monopoly ruling and soft remedies ([[antitrust-us-v-google]]); the snake-eats-tail paradox
8. **The dead internet spiral** — model collapse + AI slop ([[model-collapse-and-ai-slop]]); the licensing resistance ([[licensing-resistance]])

## Headline findings from the double deep-dive

1. **This explainer is unusually accurate.** Of 16 pre-registered claims adversarially verified against primary sources, roughly half CONFIRMED exactly (SparkToro percentages, all ten Pew numbers, Raptive $2B + McCollum quote, SISTRIX Germany 265M clicks/month, the antitrust timeline, RSL's existence and design) and most of the rest PARTIAL on nuance only. Four real corrections survive: see [[caveats-and-corrections]]. For a genre (mass-market explainer) whose corpus record is fabricated exam names and invented quotes, this is the cleanest source the pipeline has processed.
2. **Reality has already outrun the video — in the pessimistic direction.** The video (June 2026) is built on 2024-2025 sources. By its upload date: zero-click was 68% not 58.5% (SparkToro 2026), AI Mode — with **93% zero-click** — had been the *default* Google Search experience for a month (May 19, 2026, I/O), global publisher Google traffic was down 33% YoY (Chartbeat), and a German court had just made Google legally liable for false AI Overviews (June 12, 2026). See [[state-of-play-2026]].
