## One-sentence thesis

In the AI era, the durable, appreciating skill is **tư duy hệ thống / system thinking** — architecture, design, and the mental *theory* of how the parts fit — because **AI replaces typing, not thinking**. Prompting is the cheap layer that keeps getting cheaper; system thinking is the layer that keeps getting scarcer and more valuable.

## The spine of the talk (and where it comes from)

- The intellectual anchor, stated at [3:14], is a 1985 essay the speaker credits to a "lỗi lạc Danish computer scientist" (garbled auto-caption: *"Peter Law … Programming Story Building"*). This is **Peter Naur — "Programming as Theory Building" (1985)**. See [[naur-programming-as-theory-building]].
- Naur's claim — *code is not the program; the program is the theory living in the developers' heads; code is only its shadow* — is restated throughout as the reason AI can generate code yet cannot own the system.
- Metaphor: **code is the instrument, the system is the music, the developer is the conductor (nhạc trưởng).** AI plays any instrument; it never replaces the conductor.
