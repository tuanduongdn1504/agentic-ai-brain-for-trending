> **Scovillain as a star, not a footnote:** it also appears as a minor support piece on the [Primarina Trick Room team](primarina-trick-room-team.html); here the whole team is built around it.

## Identity: a burn-everything wall that sets up a Belly Drum sweep

**Mega Scovillain's** ability **Spicy Spray** burns *anything that damages it* — contact move or ranged, it doesn't matter. Since burn saps 1/16 HP a turn **and halves physical damage**, and most of the meta's scariest Pokémon are physical, a bulky Scovillain (max HP + physical Defense) becomes a wall that punishes the whole board just for attacking it. Its Grass/Fire typing also makes it **immune to powder moves** (Grass) *and* **immune to burn** (Fire). It's not a damage dealer — it's an **enabler**: **Rage Powder** pulls the opponent's attacks onto itself, buying a free turn for the real win-con to set up.

That win-con is **Belly Drum Azumarill**. Scovillain soaks a turn with Rage Powder while Azumarill uses **Belly Drum** (halve HP, max Attack); with **Huge Power** doubling its Attack and a **Sitrus Berry** healing back the Belly Drum HP, a +6 **Aqua Jet** becomes a priority nuke that sweeps regardless of Azumarill's low Speed.

## The core combo, step by step

1. **Lead Scovillain + Azumarill**, Mega-evolve Scovillain.
2. **Scovillain uses Rage Powder** — the opponent's single-target attacks are dragged onto Scovillain (who burns whoever hits it and tanks it with its physical bulk).
3. **Azumarill uses Belly Drum** on the free turn → +6 Attack; it drops to half HP, which triggers **Sitrus Berry** to heal 25% back.
4. **+6 Huge Power Aqua Jet** (priority) cleans up — it moves first despite Azumarill's low Speed, and even a 40-BP move is lethal at that Attack.
- **Knock Off** covers the priority-blockers: **Farigiraf (Armor Tail)** and **Tsareena (Queenly Majesty)** stop Aqua Jet, so Azumarill hits them with a non-priority Knock Off (and strips their items). Grass-type walls (Sinistcha) get **Overheat** from Scovillain instead.

## Supporting cast

- **Mega Raichu (Y) — the always-hit nuke (2nd Mega).** **No Guard** makes its moves never miss, so **Zap Cannon** (full paralysis) and **Focus Blast** always land. It threatens **Charizard, Staraptor, and Archaludon** — the three Pokémon that scare Mega Scovillain — and leads well with Talonflame (speed + a Will-O-Wisp to cripple the enemy's physical attacker).
- **Sylveon** — reliable Fairy damage (Hyper Voice / Moonblast) **without costing a Mega slot**, so you can commit the Mega to Scovillain (or Raichu).
- **Talonflame** — **Gale Wings** gives its Flying moves +1 priority at full HP, making it a fast **Tailwind** setter and a clean answer to **Sneasler and Whimsicott** (the fast/anti-redirect threats this team fears). **Will-O-Wisp** burns physical attackers to protect Azumarill/Sylveon; **Wide Lens** shores up Dual Wingbeat/Will-O-Wisp accuracy; **Flare Blitz** handles Steel/Ice/Grass.
- **Garchomp** — Talonflame's attacking partner: Earthquake + Rock coverage, while Talonflame's Flare Blitz answers the Ice moves Garchomp fears; Talonflame is even faster than Mega Froslass, threatening snow teams.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Whimsicott is the nightmare.** Being Grass, it **ignores Scovillain's Rage Powder**, and it can **Tailwind + Encore** — Encore-locking a post-Belly-Drum Azumarill into a non-attacking move neutralizes your whole plan. Talonflame is the designated answer.
- **Belly Drum is all-or-nothing.** Halving Azumarill's HP with no payoff (blocked priority, an Unnerve **Aerodactyl** that disables the Sitrus Berry, or an Encore) can lose you the game.
- **Scovillain deals little itself.** It's a support/wall; if the Azumarill/Raichu/Sylveon damage doesn't convert, Scovillain won't carry.
- **Rain/Hyper-offense** can rush you before the setup comes online (from the gameplay: rain and fast Hyper teams were the trickier matchups).

## Key Takeaways

- **Spicy Spray Mega Scovillain burns anything that hits it** (contact or ranged) — burn halves physical damage, so a bulky Scovillain walls the physical-heavy meta and pulls attacks with **Rage Powder**.
- **The payoff is Belly Drum Azumarill:** Rage Powder buys the turn, Sitrus refunds the Belly Drum HP, and +6 **Huge Power Aqua Jet** sweeps in priority.
- **Knock Off / Overheat** handle the answers to that plan (Armor Tail / Queenly Majesty priority-blockers; Grass walls).
- **Dual-Mega with No-Guard Mega Raichu Y** covers Scovillain's scariest checks (Charizard / Staraptor / Archaludon) with always-hit Zap Cannon + Focus Blast.
- **Whimsicott is the hard counter** (ignores Rage Powder, Encore-locks Azumarill) — Talonflame is on the team specifically to answer it.
