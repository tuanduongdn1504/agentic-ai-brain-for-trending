## Identity: lose the Speed race on purpose, win the turn anyway

Victrelian's thesis, spelled out in a mini-lesson at the start of the video: **priority beats Speed.** Move priority runs from **+5 to −7**; a higher-priority move always goes first regardless of Speed. So instead of racing, this team is **deliberately slow** and simply **stacks priority moves** so its attacks land first anyway:

- **Fake Out ×2** (Rillaboom *and* Sneasler) — **+3 priority** double-flinch to open, substituting for Speed control.
- **Grassy Glide** (Rillaboom, **+1** in Grassy Terrain) · **Sucker Punch** (Kingambit, **+1**) · **Ice Shard** (Mega Baxcalibur, **+1**) · **Extreme Speed** (Hisuian Arcanine, **+2**, the fastest hit on the team).
- And where priority isn't enough, a **Grassy-Seed Unburden Sneasler** just **doubles its own Speed**, plus **Coaching / Helping Hand** to buff the slow nukes.

The glue is a **Grassy-Terrain** engine (Rillaboom's Grassy Surge) that powers Grassy Glide, heals the grounded team, halves Earthquakes, and **switches on Sneasler's Grassy Seed** (→ Unburden). And unlike this team's Trick-Room-adjacent Baxcalibur siblings, doubled-Speed Sneasler is *wanted* here — the whole plan is to move first, not last.

## The pieces

- **Rillaboom (Grassy Surge, Miracle Seed).** The engine. Grassy Surge sets the terrain that gives **Grassy Glide** its +1 priority, heals the team, halves enemy Earthquakes, and turns on Sneasler's Grassy Seed. **Miracle Seed** boosts its Grass moves 20% (so Grassy Glide / Wood Hammer hit harder); **Fake Out** is half of the double-Fake-Out lead; **U-turn** pivots.
- **Mega Baxcalibur (Thermal Exchange).** The slow nuke — **base ~175 Attack**, and **Thermal Exchange** means it *can't be burned* and gains **+1 Attack when hit by a Fire move** (so Fire attackers feed it). **Glaive Rush** is the Dragon sledgehammer, **High Horsepower** hits Steel (the Kingambit mirror), and **Ice Shard** gives even this slow monster a **+1 priority** finisher that fits the theme.
- **Sneasler (Unburden, Grassy Seed).** The tempo piece. In Grassy Terrain the **Grassy Seed** gives +1 Def and is consumed → **Unburden doubles its Speed** (here that's a *feature*, not a conflict — the team wants to be fast). It's the **second Fake Out**, a **Close Combat / Dire Claw** attacker, and carries **Coaching** to hand an ally +1 Atk/+1 Def.
- **Kingambit (Defiant, Occa Berry).** The priority breaker. **Sucker Punch** (+1) punishes attackers; **Kowtow Cleave** is its never-miss Dark STAB; **Iron Head** hits Fairies. **Defiant** turns any enemy stat-drop (Intimidate especially) into **+2 Attack**, and the **Occa Berry** cushions the Fire hit that its Dark/Steel typing fears (2× Fire).
- **Hisuian Arcanine (Rock Head, Focus Sash).** The hardest hitter and the fastest priority. **Extreme Speed** is a **+2** revenge move; **Flare Blitz** is **recoil-free** thanks to **Rock Head**; **Rock Slide** adds spread + flinch. **Focus Sash** guarantees it gets its hit off.
- **Clefable (Unaware, Sitrus Berry).** The support spine. **Follow Me** redirects a targeted Fighting move away from Baxcalibur/Kingambit; **Life Dew** heals the team; **Helping Hand** super-charges a priority nuke; **Moonblast** is STAB. **Unaware** makes Clefable ignore an opponent's stat boosts — a clean answer to Bulk Up / Calm Mind sweepers.

## Game plan

1. **Open with double Fake Out.** Lead Rillaboom + Sneasler, Grassy Surge sets terrain (Sneasler's Grassy Seed → Unburden), and two Fake Outs buy a free turn while you set up the board.
2. **Stack priority, not Speed.** You will usually be slower — that's fine. Grassy Glide, Sucker Punch, Ice Shard, and Extreme Speed all hit first anyway; pick the one that KOs.
3. **Bring the nuke behind the priority.** Mega Baxcalibur's Glaive Rush / High Horsepower cleans up what priority chips; Coaching or Helping Hand turns a 2HKO into a KO.
4. **Use Clefable to neutralize setup and protect the frail Steels.** Unaware ignores their boosts; Follow Me eats the Fighting move aimed at Kingambit; Life Dew keeps the team topped.
5. **Kingambit closes.** As the game trades down, a **Sucker Punch** off a Defiant-boosted Attack finishes — and the Occa Berry lets it eat one Fire hit on the way. (Note this Kingambit runs **Defiant**, not the usual Supreme Overlord — it's here to punish Intimidate, not to snowball off faints.)

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Glaive Rush is a double-edged nuke.** After Baxcalibur uses Glaive Rush, **incoming attacks hit it for double and never miss until its next turn** — devastating on a slow Pokémon (Champions behavior is identical to Gen 9). Don't fire it into a faster answer you can't OHKO; Ice Shard / Protect are the safer priority turns. This is the guide's single biggest play-around.
- **Sucker Punch is conditional.** It **fails** if the target uses a status move, switches, or Protects — so a savvy opponent plays around Kingambit's main priority.
- **Ground is the shared soft spot.** **Hisuian Arcanine is 4× weak to both Ground and Water**, and **Kingambit is 2× Ground** — one strong single-target Ground move (note: Grassy Terrain *halves* Earthquake/Bulldoze, but **not** High Horsepower) pressures two members at once. Kingambit is also **4× Fighting**, and a fast Fairy that outruns the priority stack punches through the Dragon Baxcalibur. Clefable's Follow Me + Unaware, the Occa Berry, and Grassy Terrain's Earthquake-halving patch some of this — not all.
- **Priority has hard counters.** **Psychic Terrain, Queenly Majesty / Armor Tail / Dazzling, and Quick Guard all shut off your priority** — against those the "no Speed" plan stalls, and you're a slow team with no Speed control. Victrelian's own intro lists these as the answers to the strategy.
- **Ladder Bo1, four games.** A fun, coherent build shown winning on ladder — not a proven tournament result.

## Key Takeaways

- **The whole team is a priority stack:** Fake Out ×2 (+3), Extreme Speed (+2), and Grassy Glide / Sucker Punch / Ice Shard (+1) let a **deliberately slow** team move first — "no Speed but still shocks." Priority beats the Speed race.
- **Grassy Terrain does triple duty** — Grassy Glide priority, team healing + halved Earthquakes, and it **switches on Sneasler's Grassy Seed → Unburden** (doubled Speed, *wanted* here, unlike a Trick Room build).
- **Mega Baxcalibur (~175 Atk, Thermal Exchange) is the slow nuke** that even gets a priority move (Ice Shard) — but **respect Glaive Rush's double-damage-taken drawback**.
- **Defiant Kingambit + Unaware Clefable are the meta answers** — Defiant punishes Intimidate spam, Unaware ignores setup sweepers, Occa Berry cushions Fire; Follow Me + Life Dew hold the frail core together.
- **The topic's 3rd Mega Baxcalibur, and a priority-themed cousin of [[pokemon-champions-team-building/baxcalibur-grassy-imprison-team|ChampBraeden's Grassy build]]** — same Baxcalibur + Grassy Rillaboom + Grassy-Seed Unburden Sneasler (Coaching) + Rock-Head Focus-Sash Hisuian Arcanine, but swapping the Imprison-Farigiraf/Gholdengo anti-TR shell for a **Kingambit + Clefable priority package**.
