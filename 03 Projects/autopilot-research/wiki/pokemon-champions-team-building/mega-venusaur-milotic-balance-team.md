## Identity: a single-Mega wall that only fears two Pokémon

The whole team is built to let **Mega Venusaur** shine. Its **Thick Fat** ability halves Fire *and* Ice damage, which **erases Venusaur's two big weaknesses** — so as a Grass/Poison type its *only* remaining super-effective weaknesses are **Flying and Psychic**. In this meta that's basically **two relevant threats: Staraptor and Delphox.** Neutralize those and Mega Venusaur is a concrete wall that Giga-Drains itself healthy while chipping the field, and it matches up well into sand (Tyranitar/Excadrill), rain (Archaludon), and even sun.

**Milotic is the designated partner** precisely because it answers those two threats. So the pair covers each other completely: whatever beats Venusaur, Milotic handles — and the author's claim is that **the two of them alone can take down whole teams**.

## How the pieces fit

- **Milotic covers Venusaur's only holes.** Its **Competitive** turns an enemy **Intimidate into +2 Special Attack**, so a **Muddy Water** takes ~70% off a Staraptor in one hit. **Icy Wind** is the team's speed control — even a Mega Venusaur (which shares Milotic's slow-ish tier) can then out-pace a Garchomp and finish it with Giga Drain, and Icy Wind itself is 4× on Garchomp. **Coil** patches Milotic's weak Physical Defense so it never falls. (This is the same **Coil + Competitive Milotic** tech that shows up on the [Zoroark team](zoroark-illusion-fun-team.html).)
- **Incineroar — the glue.** Intimidate to blunt physical attackers, **Fake Out** + **Parting Shot** to pivot and reposition, and **Darkest Lariat** (ignores the target's Defense boosts) for reliable Dark coverage.
- **Dragonite — the physical punch.** The team takes special hits well but needed physical damage. **Life Orb + Inner Focus** (chosen over Multiscale — Life Orb recoil breaks Multiscale, and Inner Focus also means it can't be Intimidated or flinched): **Extreme Speed** is a huge priority nuke, Dragon Claw hits hard, and **Low Kick** clobbers heavy Steels/Rocks (Kingambit, Tyranitar) and does ~70% to Archaludon.
- **Kleavor — the surprise lead.** **Choice Scarf + Sharpness** (which boosts slicing moves 50%) on a 187-Attack Bug/Rock. **Stone Axe** is 4× on Charizard-Y *and* sets **Stealth Rock**, so it wrecks sun/Charizard teams and punishes pivot-heavy teams; **Night Slash** shreds the Ghosts (Gholdengo, Basculegion, Sinistcha); **Aerial Ace** never misses (anti-minimize, and OHKOs a non-Scarf Sneasler); **U-turn** pivots to bring Venusaur/Milotic/Dragonite in safely.
- **Weavile — the disruptor.** Blazing base-125 Speed with **Ice Shard** priority that 4×-revenge-kills Garchomp *through* Tailwind/speed boosts, **Knock Off** to strip items, and **Icicle Crash** as its hard-hitting Ice STAB (30% flinch). The author runs **Protect over Fake Out** as a mind-game — opponents expect Weavile to Fake Out (or to protect *against* the expected Fake Out), so he just attacks and steals turn-one tempo.

## Game plan: position, chip, and wall

This is a **positioning team**, not a fast-kill team. The two pivots — **Kleavor U-turn** and **Incineroar Parting Shot** — let you scout the opponent and drop the right answer onto the field. **Stealth Rock** from Kleavor's Stone Axe chips everything that switches, Intimidate and Icy Wind slow the board, and the **Venusaur + Milotic** core just refuses to die while Giga Drain and Muddy Water grind the opponent out. Sleep Powder is the tempo-swing: Venusaur is bulky enough to fish for it 2–3 times.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Sleep Powder is 75%** — a key tool, but RNG; don't build the whole turn around it landing.
- **Everything except Kleavor and Weavile is slow** — the team leans on Icy Wind + priority (Extreme Speed, Ice Shard) for speed control; a fast team that ignores hazards can rush you.
- **Flying/Psychic still threaten Venusaur** — if Milotic is gone, Staraptor/Delphox reopen the hole the whole team is built to cover.
- **One Mega only** — no dual-Mega flex here; Venusaurite is the single Mega, so the plan is committed.

## Key Takeaways

- **Thick Fat Mega Venusaur has only two real weaknesses** (Flying/Psychic → Staraptor/Delphox), and **Milotic is built to answer exactly those** — the core covers itself.
- **Competitive + Coil Milotic** punishes Intimidate (+2 SpA Muddy Water) and walls physically; **Icy Wind** is the team's speed control (and 4× on Garchomp).
- **Two surprise picks carry the tempo:** Scarf **Kleavor** (Sharpness Stone Axe = 4× Charizard-Y + Stealth Rock, U-turn pivot) and **Weavile** (priority Ice Shard revenge + Knock Off + Icicle Crash, Protect-over-Fake-Out mind-game).
- **Dragonite Inner Focus + Life Orb** supplies the physical punch (Extreme Speed priority, Low Kick into Steels).
- **It's a positioning/wall team** — pivot to the right answer, chip with Stealth Rock, and grind behind the Venusaur/Milotic core.
