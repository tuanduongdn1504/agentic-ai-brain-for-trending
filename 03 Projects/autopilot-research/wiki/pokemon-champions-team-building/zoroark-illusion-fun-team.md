# Guide — Zoroark "Illusion" fun/troll team (the honest anti-thesis)

> **Source:** [Master VGC: Zoroark và bá chủ thực sự của ảo ảnh | Pokemon Champions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7O93l5Ce13I) — `7O93l5Ce13I`, **Thế Giới Anime** (Vietnamese livestream), 2026-06-30, **2:03:46**. A ladder stream, not a tournament report: the creator builds a Zoroark "illusion" team live and plays it up the ranks.
> **Validated in PokéSynergy** — all six imported clean (1 warning only: the Basculegion item "Choice Band" isn't in the tool's item DB). Forms/abilities/moves confirmed in-tool; **mechanics independently fact-checked** (adversarial verify + Bulbapedia/Serebii).
> **Read this one for contrast:** it's the topic's first **fun/gimmick build** — explicitly a "cho vui" (for-fun) team that the creator says **"thắng thua không quan trọng"** (win/loss doesn't matter) and that **lost several of the shown games**. It's the honest counter-weight to the tournament-proven Steelix/Arcanine builds.

## Identity: a snow/veil core wearing a disguise

Underneath the gimmick this is a **snow + dual-screen** team. **Mega Froslass** (Snow Warning) sets snow on entry → **Blizzard is 100% accurate**, **Aurora Veil** goes up (halves physical *and* special damage for 5 turns), and every Ice-type gets +50% Defense. That's the real engine.

The **identity**, though, is **Zoroark's Illusion**: it enters battle **disguised as the last Pokémon in your party** — same sprite, name, gender, even shininess — and the disguise only drops when Zoroark is hit by a damaging move. So the opponent teambuilds and clicks against the *wrong* Pokémon. The signature play: a Zoroark disguised as the dog (Arcanine) fires **Focus Blast into Kingambit** — who never expects a Fighting move from what looks like a teammate, and (per the live meta data) almost never holds a Fighting-resist berry.

## How the pieces actually fit

- **Snow engine (Mega Froslass).** Mega-evolving sets snow (Snow Warning) and *overwrites the opponent's weather* — the creator uses this to erase enemy sun/rain mid-game. Snow → Blizzard never misses, Aurora Veil works, Ice-types tank harder. (Gen 9 snow deals **no** chip damage, unlike old Hail.)
- **The Kingambit problem, solved twice.** The core is scared of **Kingambit** — its Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave maul the Ice/Ghost Froslass and Dark Zoroark. Fighting is **4× super-effective** on Kingambit (Dark ×2, Steel ×2), so the team packs *two* Fighting answers: **Sneasler** (base **120** Speed, Close Combat = clean OHKO) and **Arcanine** (Intimidate + Close Combat). The creator explains he deliberately **does not run Kingambit himself** — it would become the team's centre of gravity and bury the Zoroark gimmick.
- **Zoroark = expression, not power.** Illusion is a mind-game, not damage. Disguised as the dog, Zoroark baits Fighting/Rock moves the real Arcanine would fear, or lands a surprise Focus Blast on Kingambit. It doesn't get STAB on Shadow Ball or Focus Blast (Dark type) — those are pure coverage/surprise tools.
- **Milotic, the "student Coil" set.** **Competitive** turns enemy Intimidate into a **+2 Special Attack** boost, so Milotic loves the Intimidate-heavy meta. The gimmicky **Coil** (legal on Milotic in Gen 9 — raises Atk / Def / accuracy) + **Recover** + **Muddy Water** (30% to blind the foe) makes it an annoying bulk-up wall.
- **Basculegion, the closer.** **Last Respects** starts at 50 BP and gains **+50 for every fainted ally** — on a fun team that trades bodies, it snowballs into a nuke. **Adaptability** doubles STAB, and **Aqua Jet** mops up in priority.

## The honest assessment (why this is a "fun" team)

The creator is blunt: this is a **fun team**, expect to **lose a lot**, and *"quan trọng gì thắng thua — vui là được"* (who cares about winning, having fun is the point). The stream bears it out — the Zoroark repeatedly **failed to trick anyone** (*"vẫn chưa lừa được ai"*) and whole games ended with *"Zorat cư game này phế quá"* (Zoroark was useless this game). The Illusion bluff pays off only against opponents who misread it; a player who thinks before moving simply plays the board correctly and the disguise adds nothing.

**Verified caveats worth knowing before you copy it:**

- **Illusion is fragile.** It breaks the instant Zoroark takes *any* damaging hit — including spread moves (Rock Slide, Blizzard, Earthquake) and a chip from an ally-target spread. In Doubles that's often turn one.
- **Two Focus Sashes, one snow.** Sash on both Zoroark and Sneasler helps the bluff/OHKO plan, but neither survives a second hit, and Aurora Veil is your only bulk once snow ticks down.
- **The "surprise Kingambit" read is meta-dependent.** It works *because* the live PokéSynergy data shows the common Kingambit set is Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave / Iron Head / Protect / Low Kick / Swords Dance — **no Chople (Fighting-resist) berry**. If berries come back into fashion, the surprise Focus Blast stops being a kill.

## Not a Game Plan Assistant preset (on purpose)

The topic's [Game Plan Assistant](steelix-synergy-guide.html) presets are all **"read the enemy, pick your mode"** teams (Steelix Sand/TR, Tailwind Snow-HO, Arcanine Speed-Flex). This team is the opposite: a **fixed gimmick** whose plan ("disguise, bluff, hope they misread") doesn't branch off the opponent's archetype — so it isn't a preset. It earns its place in the topic as the **honest low end of the spectrum**: maximum expression, minimum consistency.

## Key Takeaways

- **It's a snow/veil team in a costume.** The engine is Mega Froslass Snow Warning → Blizzard + Aurora Veil; Zoroark's Illusion is the *identity*, not the power.
- **Illusion = appear as your last party slot, break on damage.** Great for a one-time bluff (surprise Focus Blast on Kingambit), unreliable as a plan.
- **Two Fighting answers for one Kingambit.** Sneasler (base-120, 4× Close Combat OHKO) + Arcanine (Intimidate + Close Combat) cover the core's biggest fear — and the creator refuses to run Kingambit himself so it won't steal Zoroark's spotlight.
- **Fun ≠ consistent, and that's the lesson.** The creator openly loses games with it. This is the topic's benchmark for *"gimmick teams express, tournament teams convert."*
- **Milotic can legally run Coil** (Gen 9) — Competitive + Coil + Recover is a real, if cheeky, anti-Intimidate wall.
