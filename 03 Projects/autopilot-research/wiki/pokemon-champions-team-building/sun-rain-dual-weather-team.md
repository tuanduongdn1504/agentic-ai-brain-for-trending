# Guide — Sun + Rain dual-weather team (the two-weathers-in-one build)

> **Source:** [Mình Thử Đội Hình Nắng & Mưa Kết Hợp Đã Vô Địch Giải Đấu Poké Champions Với Hơn 4000 Người Tham Dự](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HKw_O0DTeNE) — `HKw_O0DTeNE`, **Duy Plays Poké** (Vietnamese), 2026-08-27, **42:25**. A **dual-weather Sun + Rain** team that put **four players into the Top 8** of the Champion Festival Core tournament (**4000+ entrants**) — a proven, heavily-piloted list.
> **Validated in PokéSynergy** — all six resolved clean (Charizard, Venusaur = **Chlorophyll**, Pelipper = **Drizzle**, Archaludon, Basculegion = **Adaptability**, Grimmsnarl = **Prankster**); every ability + move recognized, only one filler item string (Archaludon's) went unrecognized. Mechanics adversarially fact-checked; **one framing refined** (Basculegion runs Adaptability *by choice*, not because it lacks Swift Swim — see Watch-outs).
> **Two opposing weathers on one team.** Sun (Mega Charizard-Y + Venusaur) and Rain (Pelipper + Archaludon + Basculegion) cover *each other's* weaknesses, and you set whichever suits the matchup — beating the format's single-weather teams at their own game.

## Identity: pick your weather, and flip Weather Ball with it

Dual-weather teams come in many flavors (rain+sand, sun+sand…), but **Sun + Rain** is the strongest because the two systems patch each other's holes: sun's Fire core hates Water, rain's Water core hates Grass/Electric, and each weather turns the *other's* weakness into a strength. You carry a **Sun setter (Mega Charizard-Y, Drought)** and a **Rain setter (Pelipper, Drizzle)** and choose which weather to run per opponent — so a single-weather meta team is always half-countered.

The neat tech that ties it together: **Weather Ball**. It's a Normal move that **becomes the active weather's type and doubles to 100 BP** — **Fire in sun, Water in rain**. So **Charizard's Weather Ball is a Water nuke under your Pelipper's rain**, and **Pelipper's Weather Ball is a Fire nuke under Charizard's sun**. Both weather-setters carry a coverage move that flips to exactly the type the *other* weather wants.

The cost is piloting difficulty — you have to actually understand the team, or you'll set the wrong weather at the wrong time and beat yourself.

## The pieces (how the two weathers fit)

- **Mega Charizard-Y (sun).** The premier sun attacker — **Drought** auto-sets sun, **Heat Wave** spreads, **Solar Beam** fires in one turn in sun. It's built **bulkier than usual** (HP/Def) to survive the surprise **Rock Slides** that are its classic answer. Its **Weather Ball** is the dual-weather payoff: **Water** under an ally's rain.
- **Venusaur (sun partner).** **Chlorophyll doubles its Speed in sun**, and **Sleep Powder** applies turn-one pressure. *Build note from the creator:* Sleep Powder's 75% accuracy betrayed him (he whiffed **all five** early tries), so he **swapped Focus Sash → Wide Lens** (+accuracy) and re-invested into **HP/Def** to abuse the sleep over several turns. The original build (full SpA/Speed + Focus Sash) still works if you prefer.
- **Pelipper (rain setter).** **Drizzle** sets rain; it supports with **Tailwind** (team speed) + **Wide Guard** (blocks spread moves), and its **Weather Ball is Fire** under Charizard's sun. No Protect — so it can be focus-fired down if you expose it.
- **Archaludon (the connector).** The piece that "belongs" to both weathers: a **bulky special wall** (Calm, HP/SpD) that fires **Electro Shot immediately in rain**, and simultaneously the team's **hard counter to enemy rain teams**. Speed is tuned to **112** to creep other Archaludons (and outrun key threats under Tailwind). With Grimmsnarl's screens up, it's very hard to KO.
- **Basculegion (rain cleaner).** The late-game physical sweeper under rain. It runs **Adaptability** (STAB ×2 → a monstrous **Wave Crash**) with a **Choice Scarf** for speed, plus defensive EVs to **survive a Kingambit Sucker Punch** and hit back. **Flip Turn** pivots; **Last Respects** grows as teammates faint.
- **Grimmsnarl (glue).** **Prankster Reflect + Light Screen** blunt both damage types, keeping Archaludon and the main attackers off the OHKO range. Like Pelipper, it runs no Protect (a focus-fire risk).

## Game plan — three lead modes (the creator's own)

1. **Neutral / scout** — lead **Grimmsnarl + Archaludon**. Read whether the enemy is physical or special, set the matching screen, then pivot Pelipper in and swap to the right attacker.
2. **Sun aggro** — lead **Charizard + Venusaur**. Chlorophyll doubles Venusaur's Speed, **Sleep Powder** pressures a lead, and Charizard spreads. Back it with the rain half (Pelipper + Archaludon) so you can flip weathers and abuse Weather Ball.
3. **Rain aggro** — lead **Pelipper + Archaludon**. Rain turn one → immediate Electro Shot (with its SpA boost). Back it with the sun half (Charizard/Venusaur) for weather control, or Charizard + Basculegion to clean.
4. **(Fourth option)** — lead **Grimmsnarl + Basculegion**: screens up, then **Flip Turn** pivots the Choice-Scarf cleaner into the right spot.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Two of your six have no Protect** — **Pelipper and Grimmsnarl** can be **Fake-Out + focus-fired** for a quick KO if you lead them carelessly. Position them behind screens/pivots.
- **Sleep Powder is 75%** — the creator missed five in a row; the **Wide Lens** patch helps but it's still not a plan you can rely on.
- **Duo-weather is hard to pilot** — the flexibility is the strength *and* the trap; set the wrong weather at the wrong time and you hand the game away. It "requires you to really understand the team."
- **Set refinement (independently checked, per the vault's wiki-verify rule):** a checker flagged that **Basculegion *does* have Swift Swim** in its ability pool. True — but this build **chooses Adaptability + Choice Scarf on purpose**: Adaptability doubles Wave Crash's STAB, and the Scarf (plus Tailwind) supplies the speed that Swift Swim would otherwise give. It's a power-over-auto-speed trade, exactly as the creator built it — not an error. (Everything else — the Weather-Ball type flip, Chlorophyll, Drought/Drizzle overwrite, rain Electro Shot — verified clean.)

## Key Takeaways

- **Sun + Rain on one team** — carry both a Drought (Mega Charizard-Y) and a Drizzle (Pelipper) setter and pick the weather per matchup; the two opposing weathers cover each other's weaknesses and half-counter single-weather meta teams.
- **Weather Ball is the glue** — it flips to the active weather's type (Fire in sun / Water in rain, 100 BP), so **Charizard's Weather Ball is Water in your rain** and **Pelipper's is Fire in your sun**.
- **Archaludon is the connector** — a bulky wall that fires **rain Electro Shot** *and* hard-counters enemy rain, held alive by **Grimmsnarl's dual screens**.
- **Basculegion trades Swift Swim for Adaptability + Choice Scarf** — max-power Wave Crash with Scarf/Tailwind speed, built bulky enough to eat a Kingambit Sucker Punch.
- **Tournament-proven but hard to pilot** — 4 copies made Top 8 of a 4000-player event; the flexibility is real, but managing the weather correctly is the whole skill.
