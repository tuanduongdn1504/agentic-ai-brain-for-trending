# Guide — the #1 ladder team: Mega Floette + Coaching Sneasler (dual-Mega setup/support)

> **Source:** [This is the #1 team in Pokemon Champions from last season](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pYN-aH6Qm-4) — `pYN-aH6Qm-4`, **CybertronVGC** (Aaron "Cybertron" Zang), 2026-08-16, **1:56:30**. A full *Road to Ranked* breakdown of the team that **finished rank #1** on the Champions ladder last season, **built and piloted by Jude Lee** (Singapore; Worlds 2025 Top 32), with a second player, "Guardian," reaching Top 100 on the same composition.
> **Validated in PokéSynergy** — all six resolved clean on import (Mega Floette = Fairy Aura, Mega Garchomp = Sand Force, Sneasler = Unburden). Mechanics adversarially fact-checked.
> **The tournament-proven end of the topic:** unlike the fun/gimmick Zoroark team, this is a *legitimate #1* — a bulky **setup + support** build. It's also the natural "answer team" that the [sand scouting report](meta-sand-team-scouting-report.html) says to prepare for.

## Identity: buy time, set up an unkillable Floette

This is a **dual-Mega setup/support** team. The win condition is **Mega Floette**: a physically bulky Fairy that spends the early turns clicking **Calm Mind**, then heals everything back with **Draining Kiss** (which drains 75% of the damage it deals) — once it has a couple of boosts and its **Fairy Aura** power-up, it becomes almost impossible to knock out. Everything else exists to *buy Floette setup turns*: double Fake Out (Incineroar + Sneasler), Intimidate, redirection + healing (Sinistcha), and the team's signature tech — **Coaching on Sneasler**.

The second Mega, **Garchomp**, is a flex: it patches the matchups Floette hates (Fire and Steel), and the pilot picks *which* Pokémon Mega-evolves per game. "It generally feels like a Floette team, with Garchomp coming out to patch up negative matchups."

## The engine: Calm Mind Floette, and why Coaching unlocks it

- **Draining Kiss + Calm Mind = unkillable.** Calm Mind stacks Special Attack and Special Defense; Draining Kiss heals **75%** of the damage dealt (Fairy Aura and STAB make that a big number). The video's advice: **spend multiple turns setting up** — the more Calm Minds (and Coaching boosts) you land, the more trivial the endgame.
- **Coaching is the unlock.** Sneasler's **Coaching** gives Floette **+1 Attack and +1 Defense**. That single Defense boost means big physical hits (Sneasler Dire Claw, Kingambit Iron Head) *still connect but no longer threaten a KO*, so Floette trades up and gets more setup time. Opponents often don't play around Coaching, which makes it even better.
- **The bulky-not-fast-but-fast-enough spread.** This Floette runs almost **no Special Attack** — it's HP/Defense bulk plus enough Speed to matter. Floette sits in a fiercely contested Speed tier (around Charizard and Garchomp), so the speed investment lets it out-pace those threats after a Calm Mind or two and Draining-Kiss them down before they move.
- **Support glue.** Two Fake Out users (Incineroar + Sneasler) + Sinistcha's Rage Powder redirection + Incineroar's Intimidate + Helping Hand + Sinistcha's Hospitality healing = plenty of ways to protect Floette while it boosts. Helping Hand (+50% to an ally's move) turns a +2 Floette from "chip" into real one-hit KOs.

## The dual-Mega flex (pick your Mega by matchup)

- **Default: Mega Floette.** Most games. Lead **Incineroar or Sneasler** (Fake Out) with Floette in the back, plus one of **Kingambit or Sinistcha**.
  - *Incineroar lead* = immediate **Intimidate** (stacks on Floette's physical bulk) — the default unless the opponent punishes Intimidate or has a faster Fake Out.
  - *Sneasler lead* = **faster Fake Out** — good into opposing Sneaslers, and it won't trigger a slower opponent's White Herb; Fake Out turn 1, then Protect + Coaching or pivot to Incineroar.
  - *Kingambit vs Sinistcha in the back:* Kingambit = more raw damage (Iron Head into opposing Floette, Sucker Punch priority, Kowtow Cleave); Sinistcha = redirection + healing but less damage. Bring both Fake-Out users unless a matchup clearly wants Sinistcha **and** Kingambit.
- **Garchomp mode: vs anti-Floette teams.** Mega-evolve Garchomp instead when the opponent is stacked against Floette — **Mega Delphox, Charizard-Y + Venusaur, or sand teams with Steel-types (Excadrill / Gholdengo)**. Default Garchomp lineup: Mega Garchomp + Sneasler, Incineroar + Sinistcha in back. Coaching works on Garchomp too — a single Coaching boost makes the specially-defensive Garchomp very hard to KO.
- **Two clever lines:**
  - **Mega Garchomp under Trick Room.** Mega-evolving Garchomp *lowers* its base Speed (102 → 92), so against fast teams **Sinistcha sets Trick Room** and slow Mega Garchomp cleans under it.
  - **Earthquake → Unburden.** Earthquake is a spread move and *none of this team resists Ground*, so click it next to Sinistcha — or next to **Sneasler holding a consumable berry**: the Earthquake chip pops the berry, which **activates Unburden and doubles Sneasler's Speed**. Niche, but a surprise speed-control line.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Earthquake hits your own side.** No teammate is Ground-immune, so Earthquake is a positioning move, not a free button.
- **Floette's damage is mediocre un-boosted.** Without Calm Minds (and often Helping Hand), even +2 isn't guaranteed KOs — this team *must* be allowed to set up, so it can struggle against relentless offense that denies setup turns.
- **The un-used Mega Stone is a partial dead item.** Only one Mega fires per battle; the game you Mega Floette, Garchomp's Garchompite is a non-optimal item that turn (the price of dual-Mega flexibility).

## Key Takeaways

- **The win-con is a bulky Calm Mind Floette** healed by Draining Kiss (75% drain) and powered by Fairy Aura — buy it setup turns and it becomes unkillable.
- **Coaching (Sneasler) is the innovation** — +1 Def/+1 Atk on Floette (or Garchomp) is the difference between surviving key hits and not.
- **Dual-Mega flex:** Floette by default; Mega Garchomp instead into Fire/Steel/anti-Floette teams (Delphox, Charizard-Y+Venusaur, sand).
- **Two Fake Outs + redirection + Intimidate** is the whole point of the support shell — protect the setup.
- **Slow Mega Garchomp + Sinistcha Trick Room**, and **Earthquake → berry → Unburden Sneasler**, are the spicy lines.
- Tournament-proven (#1 ladder), built by Jude Lee — the "convert" counterpart to the topic's "express" gimmick teams.
