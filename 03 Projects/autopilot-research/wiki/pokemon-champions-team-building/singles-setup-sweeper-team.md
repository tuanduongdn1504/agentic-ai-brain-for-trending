## Identity: dual-Mega, "set up once and win"

This is a **Singles** team whose whole plan is to get **one** bulky attacker a free boost, then ride it. It carries **two Mega stones** — **Mega Ampharos** and **Mega Scrafty** — and (as in every dual-Mega team) you **pick which one to Mega first, per battle, at team preview** (you can still only Mega one *per battle*). Around the two Megas sit a snowball sweeper (**Quaquaval**), a near-unkillable special wall (**Florges**), a defensive-to-offensive pivot (**Annihilape**), and a flexible **6th slot** that swaps between a **spikes lead** (Hisuian Samurott) and a **rainbow-coverage first-striker** (Galarian Slowbro).

The creator's summary line: *"if you can threaten an insta Cotton Guard [Ampharos] or a Dragon Dance [Scrafty] turn one, that might just win you the game."* Several of the shown wins came from **one** Pokémon walling or setting up and never looking back.

## The 6th slot: Samurott (tournament) ↔ Slowbro (the shared upgrade)

The team has one flex slot, and it matters which version you're looking at:

- **Hisuian Samurott** (Water/Dark) @ **Choice Scarf** — **Sharpness** — *Ceaseless Edge · Aqua Cutter · Aerial Ace · Sacred Sword*. This is the **lead the creator actually plays** in the video and the **tournament version** he was locked into. **Ceaseless Edge** is a Dark slicing move that **sets a layer of Spikes** on hit (like Kleavor's Stone Axe, but Dark), so leading it chips the whole enemy team as they switch. **Sharpness boosts every one of its moves** (Ceaseless Edge, Aqua Cutter, Sacred Sword *and* Aerial Ace are all slicing moves, +50% each), and **Sacred Sword ignores the target's stat changes** (blows past a Cotton-Guard / Bulk-Up wall). Choice Scarf makes it a revenge-killer.
- **Galarian Slowbro** (Poison/Psychic) @ **Quick Claw** — **Quick Draw** — *Shell Side Arm · Flamethrower · Ice Beam · Psychic*. This is the **6th on the shared team-ID** (the version viewers actually import) — an **upgrade that patches the team's Fairy weakness**. **Shell Side Arm** is a Poison move that auto-picks physical or special (whichever hits harder) and is **super-effective on Fairies**; Flamethrower covers Steel/Fairy Mawile, giving "rainbow" special coverage. Its gimmick: **Quick Draw (30%) stacks with Quick Claw (20%)** for **~44% chance to move first** despite dead-last Speed.

The importable list below is the **Slowbro (shared) version** because that's what the team-ID distributes and what PokéSynergy validated; Samurott's set is included as a commented swap.

## How it plays (the pieces, from the shown battles)

- **Florges is the MVP wall.** In the first showcased game it hard-walled a **Primarina** (survived a Moonblast trivially — "took 40 damage after Leftovers, then a free Calm Mind"), then walled **Mega Delphox** and **Archaludon** too — *"Mega Delphox, Archaludon, Primarina, completely walled"* → free forfeit. That's the payoff of base **154 Special Defense** + Calm Mind: it eats special attackers all day, and **Psychic Noise** stops them healing back.
- **Double Intimidate makes Mega Scrafty unhittable-by-physical.** Because Intimidate re-triggers **on Mega Evolution**, you switch Scrafty in (Intimidate #1) and Mega-evolve (Intimidate #2) for **−2 Attack** on the foe. On stream, a **Life-Orb Dragapult Phantom Force** did **19 damage** through the double drop — which is exactly why the set runs **Dragon Dance over Bulk Up**: it already doesn't take physical damage, so it wants *offense*, not more defense.
- **Quaquaval snowballs.** **Aqua Step** gives **+1 Speed** every time it connects and **Moxie** gives **+1 Attack** on every KO, so one kill starts a runaway sweep; **Focus Sash** guarantees it survives one hit to get going (in Singles that's a real free turn), **Triple Axel** breaks Sashes / Multiscale / Disguise, and **Aqua Jet** mops up faster threats.
- **Mega Ampharos is the bulky breaker.** **Cotton Guard** (+3 Def) tanks physical hits while **Charge** (+1 SpD *and* doubles the next Electric move) sets up a **Parabolic Charge** that hits like a truck **and heals 50%** — a self-sustaining wall-breaker. **Mold Breaker** ignores the foe's ability, and **Dragon Pulse** is the second STAB (Mega Ampharos is Electric/**Dragon**).
- **Annihilape is the defensive-to-offensive pivot.** **Defiant** punishes any stat-drop (+2 Atk), **Bulk Up** stacks defense + attack, and **Rage Fist** starts at 50 BP and **grows +50 for every hit it has taken** (cap 350) — so the longer it tanks, the harder it hits. **Earthquake** is the Toxapex/Steel answer; **Drain Punch** keeps it healthy. On stream it survived a Charizard Weather Ball on **Sitrus Berry** and kept a Rage-Fist boost.

## Game plan (leading + the Mega pick)

1. **Pick your Mega at team preview** — Ampharos (bulky setup) or Scrafty (Dragon-Dance sweep), whichever the enemy team invites. You can only Mega one per battle, so decide *before* leading.
2. **Samurott is the default lead** — Ceaseless Edge lays **Spikes** turn one, and it's a strong Scarf attacker. **But don't autopilot it:** if a Mega has a great matchup, lead the Mega instead ("an insta Cotton Guard or Dragon Dance can win turn one").
3. **Watch for counter-leads.** A **Choice-Scarf Meowscarada** answers your Samurott lead — the creator's plan there is **Scrafty** (Scrappy/Intimidate; *"we don't care what they're trying to do to us"*).
4. **Bring Florges vs special teams.** Against a special-attacker core (Primarina / Delphox / Archaludon), Florges alone can wall the whole side while your others clean up.
5. **Set up once, then close.** The team has *four* things that snowball (Scrafty DD, Ampharos Cotton-Guard/Charge, Annihilape Bulk-Up/Rage-Fist, Quaquaval Aqua-Step/Moxie) — you only need **one** to stick.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Fairy is the team's hole — that's *why* the shared version runs Slowbro.** Mega Scrafty is **4× weak to Fairy** (Dark/Fighting) and Mega-Ampharos's Dragon half is weak too. The **tournament (Samurott) version has no Fairy answer** — the creator basically played *"with one hand tied."* The **Slowbro upgrade** exists specifically to fix this (**Shell Side Arm** is SE on Fairy, Flamethrower melts Steel/Fairy).
- **Multi-hit moves shred the setup sweepers.** A game nearly ended to a **Maushold Population Bomb** (up to 10 hits) that blew through Scrafty's bulk — it survived at **2 HP** by luck and won. Sash-breakers and multi-hitters (Population Bomb, Triple Axel mirrors) are the honest weakness of a bulk-and-boost plan.
- **Fast special Flying hurts.** Against **Charizard-Y**, an **Air Slash** set "cooks" the team; the creator flagged it as a genuinely bad matchup he plays around, not through.
- **Two verifier false-refutations, both overturned by independent check** (logged per the vault's wiki-verify rule):
  - A checker claimed **Florges's Special Defense is only 65** ("not a wall"). **Wrong** — 65 is its *Attack*; Florges's base **SpD is 154**, one of the highest in the game. The wall framing stands.
  - A checker claimed **Galarian Slowbro's abilities are Regenerator / Curious Medicine**, so "Quick Draw doesn't exist on it." **Wrong** — Curious Medicine is Galarian *Slowking*; Galarian *Slowbro*'s signature ability **is Quick Draw** (+ Own Tempo / Regenerator hidden), and Quick Draw + Quick Claw is exactly the ~44%-first-strike tech the creator built around.

## Key Takeaways

- **This is the topic's first *Singles* team** — 1v1, so the plan is **setup + walls + hazards + first-strike RNG**, not Fake Out / redirection. It's the clean contrast to every Doubles entry here.
- **Dual-Mega, pick per battle:** Mega Ampharos (Cotton-Guard/Charge/Parabolic-Charge bulky breaker) *or* Mega Scrafty (**double Intimidate** + Dragon Dance sweeper). One Mega per battle; choose at preview.
- **Florges is the anchor** — base **154 SpD** + Calm Mind + Psychic Noise walls entire special cores (Primarina/Delphox/Archaludon "completely walled" on stream). Its ability is dead weight in Singles; you run it for the stat line.
- **Set up once and win** — four independent snowball engines (Scrafty DD, Ampharos setup, Annihilape Bulk-Up/Rage-Fist, Quaquaval Aqua-Step/Moxie); you only need one to stick.
- **The Fairy hole is real and deliberately patched** — the tournament (Samurott) version is 4×-weak-Fairy with no answer; the **shared version swaps in Galarian Slowbro (Shell Side Arm)** to fix exactly that. Multi-hit moves (Maushold Population Bomb) remain the honest counter.
