## Identity: two special Megas that share one weakness — and the one that fixes it

The team is a **dual-Mega special hyper-offense** built on a single elegant idea. Its two Mega options are both **special attackers** and both **hard-walled by Sylveon**:

- **Mega Salamence** on a *special* set: **Aerilate** turns **Hyper Voice into a Flying-type spread nuke** (~108 BP, both foes), backed by **Draco Meteor** and **Tailwind** — but a **Sylveon** (Fairy, resists Dragon, enormous special bulk) "just doesn't let it exist."
- **Mega Lucario Z** — the **answer to that Sylveon**. It's a new Champions Mega: **Fighting/Steel**, a **special** attacker (Special Attack redistributed to **164**) with **Aura Guard** (halves contact-move damage, so it's bulky into physical attackers). Its **Steel Beam** (140-BP Steel special, at the cost of 50% of its own HP) **OHKOs the Sylveon** that walls Salamence — Fairy is 2× weak to Steel.

So the rule the creator repeats: **against a Sylveon team you bring EITHER Lucario Z OR Salamence, never both** — and the team packs *its own* **Sylveon** as the wall that shuts down *opposing* Salamence. Around the two Megas sits a clean support core: **double Intimidate**, **Tailwind**, a **Throat Chop** to silence enemy Hyper Voice, Grassy-Terrain priority, and a Focus-Sash Basculegion cleaner.

## The pieces

- **Mega Lucario Z (Aura Guard, special).** The star and the Sylveon-breaker. **Aura Guard halves contact-move damage**, so it can switch into physical attackers and set up **Nasty Plot**; then **Aura Sphere** (never-misses) + **Flash Cannon** are the STABs, and **Steel Beam** is the nuke that deletes Sylveon (and other Fairies). "If you want to do big damage with Lucario, this is the team."
- **Mega Salamence (Aerilate, special).** The other Mega and the spread threat: **Aerilate Hyper Voice** hits both foes as a boosted Flying move, **Draco Meteor** is the single-target nuke, and **Tailwind** is the team's speed control. It enters with **Intimidate** before Mega-evolving (half of the double-Intimidate). Its hard counter is Sylveon — which is exactly why you pick *one* Mega per matchup.
- **Sylveon (Pixilate).** The anchor and the team's own anti-Salamence tech — **Pixilate** turns every Normal move Fairy, so **Hyper Voice** (spread), **Quick Attack** (Fairy priority), and **Hyper Beam** (Fairy nuke) all hit off great special bulk, walling opposing Mega Salamence completely. (metalderk notes **Steel Beam** and **Wood Hammer** are how *your* team cracks an enemy Sylveon.)
- **Incineroar (Intimidate).** The glue and the second Intimidate, with a specific job: **Throat Chop** silences sound moves for 2 turns — shutting off the enemy's own **Hyper Voice** (Salamence/Sylveon). **Fake Out / Parting Shot** for tempo.
- **Rillaboom (Grassy Surge).** Terrain support — **Grassy Glide** priority, **Wood Hammer** ("one-taps things like Milotic"), Grassy-Terrain chip-heal, and a Fake Out to open. Also a secondary way to pressure Sylveon.
- **Basculegion (Adaptability, Focus Sash).** The Focus-Sash cleaner — **Adaptability** doubles STAB, **Last Respects** snowballs as allies faint, **Aqua Jet** is priority, and the Sash guarantees it lives to fire. "Basculegion is so good in this format."

## Game plan

1. **Pick your Mega by the enemy's Sylveon.** No Sylveon on their side → lead your threatening Mega freely. Sylveon present → bring **Lucario Z** (Steel Beam breaks it) and leave Salamence home, or play the long Sylveon chess with your own.
2. **Set Tailwind** with Salamence, or **Nasty Plot** with Lucario Z behind Fake Out / Intimidate, then fire spread damage.
3. **Throat Chop the sound attackers** — silence an enemy Hyper Voice (Salamence/Sylveon) to blank their spread offense for two turns.
4. **Double Intimidate + position vs Sneasler.** The team's soft spot is a bulky Sneasler; lean on two Intimidates and careful positioning rather than raw trades.
5. **Basculegion closes** on its Sash — Last Respects / Aqua Jet mop up after the Megas soften the board.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Borrowed team, guessed spreads.** The skeleton is a real tournament-winning sheet, but metalderk is explicit he didn't build it and **guessed the EVs/items** on stream (and repeatedly "forgot the tech"/Protect slots). Treat spreads/items as reconstructions; the species, roles, and key moves are solid.
- **Sylveon is a two-way wall — respect the "bring one Mega" rule.** Your Salamence can't touch an enemy Sylveon; their Salamence can't touch yours. Bring **Lucario Z** (Steel Beam) into Sylveon teams, not Salamence.
- **Sneasler walls this team.** The creator flags a bulky **Sneasler** (plus a second threat) as a real problem — you beat it by positioning around **double Intimidate**, not by out-damaging it.
- **Steel Beam is a one-shot.** It costs **50% of Lucario Z's HP even if it misses or is Protected** — a delete button, not a spam move, and it leaves Lucario Z frail afterward.
- **Aura Guard only covers *contact* moves — not all physical ones.** It halves damage from **contact** moves (so Lucario Z tanks Close Combat, U-turn, Flare Blitz, etc.), but **special attacks AND non-contact physical moves land in full** — Earthquake, Rock Slide, Make It Rain, and every special nuke ignore it. Don't switch it in expecting a blanket physical wall.

## Key Takeaways

- **The whole team is one idea: two special Megas that Sylveon walls, and the one that breaks Sylveon.** **Mega Lucario Z's Steel Beam** (140-BP Steel, self −50% HP) deletes the **Sylveon** that hard-walls **Mega Salamence** — so you bring Lucario Z into Sylveon, Salamence into everything else.
- **Mega Lucario Z is a bulky *special* Nasty-Plot sweeper** — Fighting/Steel, **164 SpA**, **Aura Guard** halving contact damage, Aura Sphere / Flash Cannon / Steel Beam. A very different Lucario from the physical Adaptability Mega.
- **Special Aerilate Salamence** — **Hyper Voice becomes a Flying spread nuke**; the same Aerilate the [[pokemon-champions-team-building/rillaboom-salamence-protect-king-team|"protect the King" team]] used *physically* (Double-Edge). Two ways to build the same new Mega.
- **The support does specific jobs** — double Intimidate + **Throat Chop** (silence enemy Hyper Voice) + Tailwind + Grassy-Glide priority + a Focus-Sash Basculegion cleaner.
