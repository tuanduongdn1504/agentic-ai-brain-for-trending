## Identity: the sand team that skips Mega Tyranitar

Most sand teams start with Mega Tyranitar. Kelch's doesn't — it runs **regular Choice Scarf Tyranitar** as the sand setter, which frees **both** Mega slots for **Mega Venusaur** and **Mega Staraptor**. The result is a **bulky, disruptive sand team** rather than a hyper-offensive one: everything survives a long time (Rotom-Wash, Mega Venusaur, Houndstone, Staraptor all carry real bulk), and it grinds you down with sand chip, Intimidate, burns, and — the signature — **Sleep Powder**.

Two Sand Rush users (**Excadrill + Houndstone**) share the single sandstorm, so once sand is up you're out-speeding almost the entire format.

## Why it's so hard to deal with

- **Sleep Powder Mega Venusaur — the star tech.** Venusaur runs **Sleep Powder over a Grass attack**, and because it's so hard to KO you often get **two or three** chances to click it. Sleep Powder is only 75% accurate, but missing *twice* is ~1-in-16 — so you can afford a miss and still land the key one. One sleep (on a Charizard, a Garchomp) can steamroll a neutral game. **Thick Fat** (halves Fire *and* Ice damage) is what keeps Venusaur alive long enough to keep clicking it.
- **Choice Scarf Tyranitar — surprise speed.** Nobody expects Scarf on Tyranitar, so it snipes: **Ice Punch OHKOs Garchomp** (4× — Ice on Dragon/Ground), Rock Slide catches Charizard. Even at 1 HP it's still valuable because the sand is already set.
- **Double Sand Rush off one sand.** Both **Excadrill** and **Houndstone** double their Speed under Tyranitar's sand — you don't need a weather setter each. If Staraptor also gets **Tailwind** up, they out-pace essentially everything.
- **Houndstone — closer + Ally Switch.** **Last Respects** grows +50 BP per fainted ally (a late-game nuke), and **Ally Switch** dodges the Close Combats and Earthquakes aimed at Tyranitar/Excadrill — swap positions and the attack whiffs. (Use it sparingly; it can fail on repeated use like double-Protect.)
- **Rotom-Wash — pivot + disruption.** Magnet-boosted **Volt Switch** chips *and* repivots into your sand/attacker; Will-O-Wisp neuters physical threats.
- **Everything is bulky.** Add Staraptor's Intimidate and Houndstone's Will-O-Wisp on top, and the team just refuses to die while sand and sleep wear you down.

## The dual-Mega flex + game plans (from Kelch's notes)

- **Mega Venusaur is the more common Mega** — it comes out in most games for the Sleep Powder + bulk. **Mega Staraptor** is the pick into physically-oriented teams (Intimidate matters) and as an endgame Close Combat threat; its **Tailwind** is a surprise speed swing that turns Excadrill/Houndstone loose.
- **Common lead: Rotom-Wash + Mega Venusaur.** Bulky, hard to break, and it threatens the Volt Switch pivot plus Sleep Powder. A good lead into **Charizard-Y teams** and into **rain**.
- **Flex the 4th pick — Excadrill vs Houndstone — by matchup.** Vs rain / an Archaludon, lean **Excadrill** (you need the raw damage to break it); otherwise Houndstone's closing power and Ally Switch shine.
- **General flow:** chip early and pivot with Rotom → get sand up → bring a primary attacker (Tyranitar / Excadrill) while **Venusaur slowly puts things to sleep**. Opposing teams fold fast once a key sleep lands.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Sleep Powder is 75% and RNG-swingy** — the plan leans on it; a bad run of misses can cost a game (Kelch's own framing).
- **Ally Switch is a read** — great the first time, much easier to punish once the opponent expects it, and it can fail on consecutive use.
- **The un-used Mega Stone is a partial dead item** — only one Mega per battle, so whichever of Venusaurite/Staraptite you don't activate isn't pulling item weight that game (the cost of the dual-Mega flex).
- **Grass-types ignore Sleep Powder** (it's a powder move) — as do Overcoat / Safety Goggles — so the sleep plan stalls against them.

## Key Takeaways

- **Sand without Mega Tyranitar** — regular Choice Scarf Tyranitar sets the sand *and* surprise-sweeps, freeing both Megas for Venusaur + Staraptor.
- **Sleep Powder Mega Venusaur is the engine of disruption** — bulky enough to click it 2–3 times; one sleep can win the game. Thick Fat keeps it alive vs Fire/Ice.
- **Two Sand Rush users (Excadrill + Houndstone)** double their Speed off one sand; Tailwind stacks even more speed control.
- **Houndstone** = Last Respects closer + Ally Switch tech; **Rotom-Wash** = Volt Switch pivot + burns.
- **Bulk + disruption over raw offense** — the whole team survives and grinds via sand, Intimidate, burns, and sleep. Tournament-proven (#1 world, Michael Kelch).
