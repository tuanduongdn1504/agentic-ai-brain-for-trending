## Identity: a Mega Venusaur core bolted to a sand engine

The star is **Mega Venusaur** (Thick Fat → only weak to Flying/Psychic), backed by a **dual-Mega** partner in **Mega Staraptor** (which sets **Tailwind** — giving the otherwise-slow Venusaur the Speed to outrun Mega Aerodactyl) and a bulky **Milotic** for speed control. Underneath sits a **sand package**: **Choice Scarf Tyranitar** sets the sand *and* snipes as a surprise sweeper, **Excadrill** doubles its Speed off it (Sand Rush), and **Gholdengo** rounds out coverage. You flex which Mega and which half of the team to bring per matchup.

## How the pieces fit

- **Mega Venusaur — the core.** Thick Fat erases its Fire/Ice weaknesses (only Flying/Psychic hurt it); **Earth Power** hits the Steels/Fires that wall Grass+Poison (Gholdengo, Heatran-likes), **Giga Drain** sustains, **Sludge Bomb** is STAB. It's built bulky with enough SpA + Speed that **Staraptor's Tailwind** pushes it past fast threats. Neat trick: keep it in **base form (Chlorophyll)** against a sun team so it's fast in *their* sun, and only Mega later.
- **Mega Staraptor — the flex Mega + speed.** Base **Intimidate** blunts physical leads; on Mega it's **Contrary**, so Close Combat's drops become boosts. Mainly here for **Tailwind** (the team's speed engine) and a Fighting/Flying attacking win-con.
- **Milotic — the annoyance.** **Competitive** punishes Intimidate (+2 SpA), **Icy Wind** is extra speed control, **Scald** burns the physical threats (Sneasler, Kingambit) to deny their power, and Recover keeps it around.
- **The sand package.** **Choice Scarf Tyranitar** is the surprise — it sets sand *and* snipes (Ice Punch OHKOs Garchomp, Rock Slide catches Charizard); **Excadrill** (Sand Rush) doubles Speed under the sand and Iron-Heads the Fairies (Mega Floette, Sylveon); **Gholdengo** (Good as Gold, Steel/Ghost so it's **sand-immune**) covers Mega Floette (Make It Rain) and rain teams (Thunderbolt into Pelipper).

## The built-in tension (an honest read)

This is a **sand team whose best Pokémon don't like sand.** Sandstorm chips 1/16 HP a turn from everything except Rock/Ground/Steel — so **Mega Venusaur (Grass/Poison), Mega Staraptor (Flying), and Milotic (Water) all take chip damage from their *own* Tyranitar's sand**, while only Tyranitar, Excadrill, and Gholdengo are immune. You're trading passive damage on your core for Excadrill's Sand Rush speed and Tyranitar's surprise Scarf. That's the "shouldn't be this good" wrinkle: the sand is a *tool for two Pokémon*, not a blanket buff — you usually bring the sand package **or** lean on the Venusaur/Tailwind core, rather than flooding sand while your Venusaur sits in it. (The creator also questions **Excadrill** itself — no STAB Rock Slide, Stomping Tantrum over it — and muses that a **Sand Rush Lycanroc** with STAB Rock Slide might hit harder.)

## Game plan (dual-Mega, pick your half)

- **Venusaur mode:** lead/support around **Mega Venusaur + Mega Staraptor Tailwind + Milotic** — the balance core, with Tailwind carrying the Speed.
- **Sand mode:** bring **Scarf Tyranitar + Excadrill** when you want the Sand Rush speed and a surprise revenge-killer; Gholdengo as the special breaker.
- **Matchup picks (from the creator's own game):** vs a Mega-Floette / Incineroar / Sneasler / Delphox team, he skipped Venusaur (poor into it) and brought **Milotic + Gholdengo + Mega Staraptor + Scarf Tyranitar** — Milotic for speed control + Competitive, Gholdengo to blow up Floette, Staraptor Tailwind, Tyranitar to snipe. Against sun, keep Venusaur base (Chlorophyll) early.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Sand hurts your own core** (see above) — don't leave Venusaur/Staraptor/Milotic sitting in your sand longer than you need Excadrill's Sand Rush.
- **It's a recreation.** Chill rebuilt a meta team and second-guesses a couple of slots (Excadrill vs Lycanroc), so treat the exact sets as a starting point.
- **Venusaur is matchup-dependent.** Great into sand/rain/most of the field, but weak into Flying/Psychic and into fast Fire spam it can't outslow — which is *why* the team carries the whole sand-offense half as an alternative.

## Key Takeaways

- **A hybrid:** the Mega Venusaur balance core (Venusaur + Milotic + Tailwind Staraptor) welded to a Scarf-Tyranitar sand package (Tyranitar + Excadrill + Gholdengo).
- **Dual-Mega, pick your half** — Venusaur + Tailwind for the grind, or commit the sand package for Sand Rush speed + a surprise Scarf revenge-kill.
- **The honest wrinkle:** three of your six take sand chip from your *own* Tyranitar — the sand is a tool for Excadrill/Tyranitar, not a free team buff.
- **Gholdengo is the sand-immune glue** — Steel typing dodges the chip, Make It Rain answers Floette, Thunderbolt answers rain.
- **Creator's own doubt:** Excadrill (no STAB Rock Slide) may be the weakest link — Sand Rush **Lycanroc** is a harder-hitting alternative.
