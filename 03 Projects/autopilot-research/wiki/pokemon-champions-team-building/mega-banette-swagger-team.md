> **Validated in PokéSynergy** — all six resolved clean (Mega Banette = Frisk→**Prankster**, Sneasler = **Unburden**, Politoed = **Drizzle**, Corviknight, Archaludon, Sylveon = **Pixilate**); every ability + move + the Banettite Mega Stone recognized. > **The engine is a combo, not a Pokémon.** Prankster **Mega Banette Swaggers its own Lum-Berry Sneasler** — +2 Attack, the Lum instantly cures the confusion, and losing the berry flips on **Unburden** to double Sneasler's Speed. One priority move turns Sneasler into a +2, blazing-fast sweeper.

## Identity: a Prankster support that hands its partner a free Swords Dance

Mega Banette is a genuinely bad Mega on paper (near the bottom of the win-rate charts). This team makes it a **Prankster support engine**. The signature line:

1. Lead **base Banette** — its **Frisk** ability reveals a foe's held item on entry (huge in best-of-one closed-sheet ladder: you learn where the Choice Scarfs and Focus Sashes are).
2. **Mega-evolve** → ability becomes **Prankster** (+1 priority on status moves).
3. **Swagger your own Sneasler.** In Doubles you *can* target an ally with Swagger — it gives **+2 Attack** (and confusion). Sneasler's **Lum Berry** immediately cures the confusion, and because the berry is now gone, **Unburden doubles Sneasler's Speed**. The result: a **+2 Attack, doubled-Speed Sneasler** that outpaces the format and picks up **turn-one KOs** — effectively a free Swords Dance *and* a Choice Scarf, from one priority move.

Around that, Banette brings a spiky toolkit — **Poltergeist** (a 110-BP Ghost move that weaponizes the *target's* held item), **Destiny Bond** (trade a KO when Banette is about to faint), and **Trick Room** — and **Politoed** closes games with **Perish Song**.

## How the pieces fit

- **Banette (the engine).** Frisk scouts items pre-Mega; Prankster then makes **Swagger** (ally boost) and **Destiny Bond** move first. **Poltergeist** does "a ton of damage even without attack investment" (it 2HKOs most of the format) — but *fails if the target has no item*, so Frisk's info tells you when it's safe. **Destiny Bond** lets a low Banette trade itself for a key threat, which flows straight into a **Politoed Perish Song** end-game.
- **Sneasler (the payoff).** After the ally-Swagger it's +2 and twice as fast. It runs **Throat Chop over Dire Claw** on purpose — Throat Chop is **Dark** (super-effective on the format's popular Ghosts: **Sinistcha, Basculegion, Gholdengo**) and blocks sound moves; **Rock Slide** threatens **Charizard**; **Close Combat** is the nuke. The cost: no Dire Claw means it's softer into Fairies — which is why the team packs a Fairy wall.
- **Corviknight (the Fairy answer + late-game).** Steel typing walls the Fairies Sneasler can't threaten; **Iron Defense → Body Press** (Body Press hits off Defense) makes it a self-sufficient win-condition in long games. **Bright Powder** is a "nothing-better-was-free" evasion item (Sitrus/Leftovers were spoken for).
- **Politoed (the closer).** **Drizzle** rain powers **Archaludon** + Weather Ball; but its real job is **Perish Song** end-games (paired with Destiny Bond / Hypnosis to force the faint). It's built slow and bulky, which also enables a **Trick Room** plan with Banette once Sneasler has done its job.
- **Archaludon (rain nuke).** Standard, but **Snarl over Draco Meteor** — the −SpA chip improves its longevity vs special attackers (Gholdengo mirrors) and greases Perish end-games.
- **Sylveon (Fairy nuke).** Classic **Pixilate** — Hyper Voice / Hyper Beam become boosted Fairy STAB, Quick Attack becomes Fairy priority.

## Game plan

1. **Lead Banette + Sneasler** most games. Mega-evolve Banette (after noting Frisk's item read), **Swagger the Sneasler**, and start taking turn-one KOs with a +2, doubled-Speed sweeper.
2. **Use Protect on Sneasler** when you want Banette to act first (Poltergeist / Destiny Bond) or to dodge a revenge-kill and pivot.
3. **Convert to a Perish end-game** — once you're ahead, **Destiny Bond** + **Politoed Perish Song** (with Hypnosis as a bail-out) closes stubborn games without needing more damage.
4. **Grind with Corviknight** vs heavy physical / Fairy teams — Iron Defense + Body Press outlasts them.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Mega Banette is frail and combo-reliant.** It's a bottom-tier Mega for a reason; the team works because of the *Swagger/Unburden engine* and Banette's support role, not raw stats. If the combo is disrupted (fast Taunt, a Sneasler revenge-KO), the team loses a lot of pressure.
- **Poltergeist fails into itemless targets** — Frisk's scouting is what keeps it honest; don't fire it blind.
- **Hypnosis is 60%** — a bail-out, not a plan; the reliable win-con is the Swagger sweep + Perish.
- **Two verifier false-refutations, independently overturned** (logged per the vault's wiki-verify rule — the team's centerpiece survived a skeptic pass):
  - A checker claimed **Swagger can't target your own ally in Doubles**. **Wrong** — Swagger targets "any adjacent Pokémon," and the *"Swagger + Lum/Persim on your partner = an effective Swords Dance without the confusion"* tech is well-documented (Nugget Bridge / Bulbapedia). CybertronVGC uses it on stream repeatedly.
  - A checker claimed **Lum Berry only cures confusion at end of turn**, breaking the combo. **Wrong** — status-cure berries trigger the moment the status is inflicted, so the confusion is cleared and **Unburden** flips on right away, exactly as the creator demonstrates (Sneasler ends up +2 and blazing fast).

## Key Takeaways

- **One priority move = a free Swords Dance + Choice Scarf.** Prankster Mega Banette **Swaggers its own Lum Sneasler** → +2 Attack, Lum cures the confusion, **Unburden** doubles Speed → a turn-one sweeper. That combo is the whole team.
- **Frisk before Mega, Prankster after** — base Banette scouts the opponent's items (great in best-of-one), then Megas into the Prankster engine.
- **Banette's toolkit punches above its stats** — **Poltergeist** (weaponize the foe's item), **Destiny Bond** (trade KOs into a **Politoed Perish Song** close).
- **Sneasler runs Throat Chop over Dire Claw** to OHKO the meta Ghosts (Sinistcha / Basculegion / Gholdengo); the Fairy gap that opens is covered by **Corviknight** (Iron Defense + Body Press wall/win-con).
- **A bottom-tier Mega reached Champion tier** — proof that a well-built *combo* beats raw Mega power ranking; the reusable lesson for the topic's off-meta thesis.
