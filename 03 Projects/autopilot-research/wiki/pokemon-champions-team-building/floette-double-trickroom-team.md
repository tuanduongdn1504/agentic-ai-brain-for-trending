> **Source:** [Master VGC: Loạn chiến cùng hoa vĩnh cửu Floette](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZHd79W0RbUU) — `ZHd79W0RbUU`, **Thế Giới Anime** (Vietnamese livestream), 2026-08-17, **2:34:50**. The streamer pilots a **Trick Room Floette** team built by another VN player, **Biện Ngọc Nhi**, to climb the Champions ladder.
> **Validated in PokéSynergy + Serebii's Champions Pokédex** — all six resolved clean; the Champions learnsets for Gourgeist (Trick Room / Poltergeist / Trick-or-Treat) and Armarouge (Trick Room / Wide Guard) were confirmed directly against Serebii's Champions pages (they differ from Scarlet/Violet). Mechanics adversarially fact-checked.
> **A second, very different Floette team:** where the [#1 ladder Floette team](floette-coaching-dual-mega-team.html) is a Coaching-Sneasler dual-Mega build, this one is a **double Trick Room** team with a signature **Trick-or-Treat Kingambit** trick.

## Identity: two Trick Room setters, a Fighting-immune Kingambit, and a Floette payoff

The plan is **Trick Room** — flip the speed order so this slow, bulky team moves first — then win with a **Calm Mind Mega Floette** that Draining-Kisses itself unkillable. What makes it *this* team are two ideas:

1. **Redundant Trick Room.** *Both* **Gourgeist** and **Armarouge** set Trick Room, so the mode is reliable to establish and re-establish even if one setter is removed.
2. **The Trick-or-Treat tech.** Gourgeist uses **Trick-or-Treat on its own Kingambit**, adding the **Ghost** type — so Kingambit (normally Dark/Steel, **4× weak to Fighting**) becomes Dark/Steel/**Ghost** and is **completely immune to Fighting** (Ghost takes 0× from Fighting, which overrides the 4× entirely). Its single biggest weakness simply disappears.

## How the engine works

- **Gourgeist-Super — the trick engine.** The slowest/bulkiest Gourgeist form. **Frisk** reveals the opponent's items (setting up **Poltergeist**, a 110-BP Ghost move that only works if the target holds an item). It **sets Trick Room**, casts **Trick-or-Treat on Kingambit** (→ Fighting immunity), and can **Ally Switch** to dodge a focused attack.
- **Kingambit — the beneficiary.** With Ghost added, it laughs at the Close Combats/Mach Punches that would 4×-KO it, and pressures with **Kowtow Cleave** (never-misses Dark STAB), **Sucker Punch** priority, and **Swords Dance** to sweep. **Defiant** punishes Intimidate with a +2 Attack spike; **Life Orb** for power.
- **Armarouge — Trick Room #2 + a nuke.** A second **Trick Room** setter for redundancy, plus **Armor Cannon** (120-BP Fire signature that drops its own Def/SpD), **Wide Guard** to blank enemy spread moves, and **Ally Switch**. **Flash Fire** shrugs off Fire attacks; **Shuca Berry** eats a super-effective Ground hit.
- **Mega Floette — the payoff.** Comes in last and slow (no Speed investment) so it moves first under Trick Room. **Calm Mind** stacks Special Attack/Defense while **Draining Kiss** (75% drain, boosted by STAB + **Fairy Aura**) heals it to full — it becomes almost impossible to KO. Moonblast handles Dragons.
- **Sneasler + Weavile — the fast half.** Two **Fake Out** users (base 120/125 Speed) to freeze threats and buy the Trick Room turn, plus priority (**Ice Shard**) and utility (**Knock Off**, Coaching, Dire Claw's status chance from **Poison Touch**). They also give the team a *fast* mode in games where you don't want Trick Room at all.

## Game plan: set the room, flip the Kingambit, sweep with Floette

The usual flow: lead a **Fake Out** user (Sneasler/Weavile) alongside **Gourgeist** — Fake Out freezes a threat so Gourgeist safely sets **Trick Room** (or **Trick-or-Treats** the Kingambit). Then Kingambit — now Fighting-immune — breaks things with Kowtow/Sucker Punch, Armarouge nukes with Armor Cannon, and **Floette** comes in last to Calm-Mind-sweep behind the room. Because **two** Pokémon carry Trick Room, you can re-set it after the first five turns rather than losing the mode.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **No Trick Room = a slow team in trouble.** The streamer flags that a slow Floette "just gets punched" if the room isn't up — the whole plan hinges on Trick Room. The two setters + Fake-Out support exist to make that reliable, but it's the dependency.
- **It's a chaotic, high-skill team.** The title literally calls it *"loạn chiến"* (chaotic battles); even the streamer was borrowing Biện Ngọc Nhi's team and still learning the lines. Lots of decisions (when to Trick-or-Treat, when to Ally Switch, TR vs fast mode).
- **The Trick-or-Treat costs a turn.** Making Kingambit Fighting-immune is powerful but it uses Gourgeist's turn — you're trading tempo for the immunity, so time it against a Fighting threat.
- **Champions-specific learnsets.** Gourgeist's Trick Room/Poltergeist and Armarouge's Trick Room/Wide Guard are **Champions** movepool options — they're *not* in the Scarlet/Violet learnsets, so don't be thrown if an SV resource says otherwise (confirmed here against Serebii's Champions Pokédex).

## Key Takeaways

- **The trick is Trick-or-Treat on your own Kingambit** — Ghost typing makes it **immune to Fighting**, erasing its 4× weakness (Ghost's 0× overrides the 4×).
- **Two Trick Room setters** (Gourgeist + Armarouge) make the mode reliable to set and re-set — this is a *committed* Trick Room team, not a one-shot.
- **Mega Floette is the win-con** — slow on purpose, it Calm-Minds and Draining-Kisses itself unkillable once the room is up.
- **A fast half (Sneasler + Weavile)** provides Fake-Out protection for the setup and a non-Trick-Room plan B.
- **Champions ≠ Scarlet/Violet learnsets** — Gourgeist Trick Room/Poltergeist and Armarouge Trick Room/Wide Guard are Champions-specific (verified on Serebii's Champions Pokédex).
