# Guide — Milotic + Mega Raichu sleep-lock (Coil → Hypnosis control)

> **Source:** [Đội Hình Với Milotic và Mega Raichu này Đã Dành Rất Nhiều Thứ Hạng Cao Tại Các Giải Đấu Lớn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4LmLeh1HgTw) — `4LmLeh1HgTw`, **Duy Plays Poké** (Vietnamese), 2026-08-22, **34:22**. A tournament-placing team whose whole win condition is a **Milotic** setup: *"sometimes you win without knocking out a single one of the opponent's Pokémon."*
> **Validated in PokéSynergy** — all six resolved clean (Milotic runs **Coil + Hypnosis**; Raichu-Mega-Y = **No Guard**; Ceruledge = Flash Fire; all items recognized). Mechanics adversarially fact-checked.
> **A control/sleep-lock archetype** — new for the topic: it wins by putting the whole enemy team to sleep, not by out-damaging it.

## Identity: Coil up, then sleep the whole board

The win condition is **Milotic**. Hypnosis is normally unreliable (60% accuracy), but Milotic learns **Coil** — which raises Attack, Defense, *and* **accuracy** by one stage each. After one Coil, Muddy Water is 100% and Hypnosis ~80%; after **two** Coils, Hypnosis effectively hits **~100%**, letting Milotic **sleep-lock the opponent's Pokémon**. From there you set up and grind them out while they're asleep. **Competitive** also turns any enemy Intimidate into a **+2 Special Attack** boost, so Milotic loves the Intimidate-heavy meta.

Everything else exists to **protect Milotic long enough to Coil twice**: a **double Fake Out** (Mega Raichu + Incineroar), Intimidate, and Sinistcha's redirection buy the setup turns.

## How the pieces fit

- **Milotic — the engine.** **Coil** is the key move: it's not for the Attack boost, it's for the **accuracy** boost that turns Hypnosis into a near-guaranteed sleep. Two Coils and Milotic locks the field asleep; Muddy Water chips, Protect stalls sleep turns, and Calm bulk lets it survive Charizard Solar Beam / Pelipper Hurricane / Fairy Hyper Voice while it sets up.
- **Mega Raichu (Y) — the flex ability piece.** In *base* form it runs **Lightning Rod** — switch it into an Archaludon **Electro Shot** to absorb the Electric move and gain +1 Special Attack. When you Mega-evolve, it becomes **No Guard**, so **Zap Cannon** (a guaranteed paralysis) and **Focus Blast** *always* hit. It also gives the team a fast **Fake Out** (before Sneasler) and threatens Pelipper (Zap Cannon) / Archaludon (Focus Blast).
- **Ceruledge — the physical closer.** **Flash Fire** hard-walls Charizard (immune to its Fire; even Solar Beam does little), and it beats the sun shells (Venusaur), Floette, and Kingambit. **Bitter Blade** deals damage *and* heals 50% back; **Shadow Sneak** is priority (into Basculegion); **Bulk Up** sets up; **Colbur Berry** lets it survive a Kingambit **Sucker Punch**.
- **Incineroar + Sinistcha — the shell.** Incineroar is the **second Fake Out** (the "double Fake Out" combo with Raichu) plus Intimidate/Parting Shot to keep lowering the opponent, and Knock Off utility. Sinistcha (Hospitality heal, Matcha Gotcha, **Rage Powder** redirection) covers the rain matchup and soaks attacks so Milotic/Ceruledge can set up.

## Game plan (the creator's lead logic)

- **Default leads:** **Milotic + Incineroar** (vs physical-heavy teams — double Intimidate + Fake Out to Coil safely) or **Milotic + Raichu** (when the enemy is slower than Raichu, or has **Sneasler** — Raichu's Fake Out beats Sneasler's).
- **vs Sun (Charizard / Venusaur / Whimsicott):** lead **Milotic + Raichu**, Ceruledge in the back (Flash Fire eats Charizard). If you suspect a Garchomp lead, bring **Incineroar** to Intimidate it and let Milotic get a free Coil behind Fake Out.
- **vs Rain (Pelipper / Archaludon / Swampert):** lead **Milotic + Incineroar**, back **Raichu + Sinistcha** — switch Raichu into Electro Shot (Lightning Rod), Zap Cannon the Pelipper, Focus Blast the Archaludon, and Sinistcha Rage-Powders around Swampert.
- **vs Trick Room:** lead **Milotic + Raichu** — take the free first Trick Room turn to Coil, and Zap Cannon to paralyze.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **It hinges on landing the Coils.** If you're forced to click Hypnosis early (before two Coils), the higher miss chance can cost you the tempo and the whole setup plan — the creator names this as the team's main weakness.
- **Milotic hates strong Electric types** — they threaten to KO the win-con before it sets up.
- **The 6th slot is unsettled** — even the creator barely uses it; treat this as a five-Pokémon core plus a flex filler, not a polished six.
- **Sleep is variance either way** — a control team leans on a status move as its plan A; against Grass-types (immune to nothing here, but note Sleep-immunity abilities like Insomnia/Vital Spirit / Safety Goggles) the lock can fail.

## Key Takeaways

- **Coil is an accuracy button, not a damage button:** it lifts Milotic's Hypnosis from 60% to ~100% over two turns, enabling a **sleep-lock** that can win without KOs.
- **The shell protects the setup** — double Fake Out (Raichu + Incineroar) + Intimidate + Sinistcha redirection buy Milotic its Coil turns.
- **Mega Raichu Y is a dual-ability flex** — base **Lightning Rod** (soak Electro Shot) → Mega **No Guard** (always-hit Zap Cannon + Focus Blast).
- **Ceruledge is the win-con vs Charizard/sun** — Flash Fire immunity + healing Bitter Blade + priority Shadow Sneak + Colbur Berry vs Kingambit.
- **Its weakness is its own plan** — miss the Coils (or click Hypnosis too early) and the control engine stalls; strong Electric types rush Milotic.
