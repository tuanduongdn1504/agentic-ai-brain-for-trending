# Guide — Samurott "Soak" rain team (type-manipulation support)

> **Source:** [This Samurott Team DOMINATES Type Matchups! | Pokémon Champions VGC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tYD7LtQ5NgA) — `tYD7LtQ5NgA`, **OhNoItsCody** (English), 2026-08-23, **27:22**. An "underused Pokémon" build showcase: **regular (Unova) Samurott** as a bulky **Soak** support in a rain team, demonstrated winning ranked ladder games.
> **Validated in PokéSynergy** — the notable picks resolved clean: **Samurott (Torrent) + Soak / Icy Wind / Hydro Pump**, **Archaludon (Stalwart) + Electro Shot**, **Raichu (Lightning Rod) + Zap Cannon**, plus Pelipper (Drizzle), Scovillain (Moody), Sableye (Prankster). Mechanics adversarially fact-checked; **two set corrections applied** (Worry Seed reassigned Sableye → Scovillain; Scovillain's "burn" is the Mega's *Spicy Spray ability*, not a move — see Watch-outs).
> **The creative hook is Soak** — a status move that turns a foe **purely Water**, which (a) makes the team's Electric + Grass damage **super-effective**, and (b) **strips the foe's own typing** (Soak an Incineroar and its Fire STAB is gone).

## Identity: rain core + a type-changing support

Underneath, this is a **classic meta rain team** — **Pelipper Drizzle** → **Archaludon Electro Shot** (no charge turn in rain) + rain-boosted Water. The twist is the support half: **regular Samurott** (the original Unova starter, usually outclassed by its Hisuian cousin) built **super bulky** with **Soak + Icy Wind**, and **Mega Scovillain** as a Moody / burn pivot. Two Megas (**Raichu Y** and **Scovillain**) — pick which to Mega per battle.

**Soak is the centerpiece.** It changes the target to **pure Water**, and everything on the team is built to punish that:
- **Electric** from **Mega Raichu Y** and **Archaludon** becomes super-effective.
- **Grass** — **Scovillain's Giga Drain** — becomes super-effective *and* heals (the combo the creator called the most fun part of the game).
- The foe **loses its own typing**: Soak an **Incineroar** and its Fire STAB is neutralized; Soak a **Sinistcha** and a Giga Drain KOs it; Soak a **Kangaskhan** and it's just a Water-type with no offense.

## The pieces (how they fit)

- **Samurott** does almost no damage — it's a pure enabler. **Soak** sets up the Electric/Grass super-effective hits and neutralizes a threat's typing; **Icy Wind** is spread speed control; **Hydro Pump** is emergency damage; it's built bulky enough to sit on the field and keep Soaking.
- **Mega Raichu Y** solves the team's biggest structural problem: **two Water types (Samurott + Pelipper) share an Electric weakness.** In base form, **Lightning Rod** *draws in* single-target Electric moves (immunity + a SpA boost), shielding both. When it Megas, **No Guard** turns **Zap Cannon** into a 100%-accurate **guaranteed paralysis** — brutal into anything Soaked.
- **Archaludon** is the rain nuke, and it runs **Stalwart** specifically so its **Electro Shot cannot be redirected** — not by an enemy Rage Powder / Follow Me, and *not by its own Raichu's Lightning Rod*. So you can fire Electro Shot straight into the target you want even with Raichu on the field. **Shuca Berry** (over Leftovers/Stamina) lets it survive a super-effective Earth Power.
- **Pelipper** sets the rain and hits hard — **Life Orb Weather Ball** (100-BP Water in rain) one-shots things like Sinistcha, and it brings **Wide Guard** (blocks spread moves) + **Tailwind** for the team.
- **Mega Scovillain** is the flex support: base **Moody** stacks random boosts while it stalls behind Protect, and once it **Mega-evolves its ability becomes Spicy Spray — it burns *anything* that hits it** (contact or not), gutting physical attackers. Its moves do the dirty work: **Giga Drain** (the Soak payoff + healing), **Worry Seed** (strip an ability — it gave a Kangaskhan **Insomnia** and erased its **Parental Bond**), and **Foul Play**.
- **Sableye** is Prankster glue — **priority Reflect + Light Screen** to blunt both attack types, with **Rain Dance** as a backup weather setter if Pelipper is gone.

## Game plan

1. **Lead the rain + a support.** Pelipper (rain) with Samurott or Scovillain covers most matchups; against Trick Room / slower teams, Scovillain + Sableye set up screens and Moody boosts while you scout.
2. **Pick your Mega for the matchup** — **Raichu Y** (Zap Cannon pressure / Lightning Rod protection) vs **Scovillain** (Moody bulk → Spicy Spray burn wall).
3. **Find the Soak angle.** Identify the foe you want to *neutralize or expose* — Soak it to strip its STAB (Incineroar's Fire, a Fairy's Moonblast typing) or to open it to a super-effective **Zap Cannon / Giga Drain**. Because Samurott is slow, the combo often works *better under Trick Room* or after an Icy Wind (Soak first, then the faster partner hits the now-Water target).
4. **Fire Electro Shot freely.** Stalwart means you never have to play around your own Raichu's Lightning Rod or an enemy redirector.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Soak is slow-tempo.** Samurott is deliberately slow, so the "Soak → super-effective hit" combo usually needs the *partner* to move after Samurott (great under **Trick Room**, or once **Icy Wind** has dropped the target). The creator notes Soak is normally run on faster builds — this is the bulky-support experiment.
- **The team leans on speed** — a dedicated Trick Room team is a "bit of a hard matchup," patched by screens + Icy Wind + Tailwind rather than solved outright.
- **Two set corrections (independently checked, per the vault's wiki-verify rule):**
  - **Worry Seed is on *Scovillain*, not Sableye.** Sableye (Dark/Ghost) cannot learn Worry Seed (it's Grass-restricted), and in the shown game Sableye had already fainted when the Worry Seed was cast — so the on-field **Scovillain** (Grass/Fire, a legal user) is who stripped Kangaskhan's ability.
  - **Scovillain's "burn" is an ability, not a move.** The creator said "burn them with Spicy Extract," but **Spicy Extract** actually raises the target's Attack +2 / lowers Defense −2 (no burn). The burn comes from **Mega Scovillain's signature ability, Spicy Spray**, which burns any attacker that hits it. *(This lightly corrects a wording slip in the topic's [earlier Scovillain guide](mega-scovillain-azumarill-team.html), which called Spicy Spray a "move" — the burn mechanic there was right; it's an ability.)*

## Key Takeaways

- **Soak is offense as type-manipulation** — turn a foe pure-Water to (a) open it to super-effective **Electric (Raichu/Archaludon)** and **Grass (Giga Drain)**, and (b) **erase its own typing** (Incineroar's Fire, a Fairy's Moonblast). The Giga-Drain-into-a-Soaked-target combo is the team's signature.
- **Regular Samurott earns its slot as a *support*, not an attacker** — very bulky, Soak + Icy Wind, minimal damage. A genuine "underused Pokémon" pick.
- **Lightning Rod + Stalwart is elegant redirection design** — Raichu *soaks up* enemy Electric (protecting the two Waters), while Archaludon's **Stalwart** lets Electro Shot **ignore all redirection**, including its own Raichu.
- **Dual-Mega:** Raichu Y (No Guard → 100% Zap Cannon + para) or Scovillain (base **Moody** → Mega **Spicy Spray** burn-wall) — pick per battle.
- **It's a speed-reliant rain team** — screens (Prankster Sableye), Icy Wind, and Tailwind patch the Trick-Room matchup rather than beating it head-on.
