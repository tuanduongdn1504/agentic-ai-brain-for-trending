## Identity: build a glass cannon, then wrap it in terrain and speed

Mega Absol Z is a **new Champions Z-Mega**: it changes Absol from mono-Dark into **Dark/Ghost**, swaps Super Luck for **Sharpness** (every "slicing" move hits 50% harder), and turns Absol into a **154-Attack, 151-Speed** monster (BST 565) that outspeeds nearly the whole format unboosted. The catch is that the Mega pours everything into offense and adds **no bulk at all** — it stays **65 HP / 60 Def / 60 SpD**, paper-thin, so a single priority move or any Fairy attack ends it. So the entire team is a **support-and-speed shell** whose job is to clear the board of Absol's answers and give it a free, fast turn to slice through everything.

The elegant part is the **typing math**: Dark/Ghost is **immune to Normal, Fighting, *and* Psychic**, and its **only** weakness is **Fairy**. That's why this Absol carries **Play Rough** — its own coverage for the one type that threatens it — alongside **Night Slash + Shadow Claw**, *both* of which are Sharpness-boosted slicing moves (Ghost STAB + a high-crit Dark move). Around it: a **Grassy-Terrain** engine (Rillaboom + a Grassy-Seed Milotic), **Tailwind + Intimidate** from a Life-Orb Salamence, a **Focus-Sash Unburden Sneasler**, and a **Choice-Scarf Trick** Gholdengo to hard-disrupt the walls that would otherwise sponge Absol.

**This is a single-Mega team.** Despite pairing Absol with Salamence, the only Mega is Absol Z — **Salamence holds a Life Orb, not a Mega Stone**, so it's a regular-Intimidate special pivot, not Mega Salamence.

## The pieces

- **Mega Absol Z (Sharpness).** The win condition. **Sharpness** boosts its slicing moves — **Night Slash** (Dark, high crit) and **Shadow Claw** (Ghost STAB) — by 50%, and at ~151 base Speed it moves before almost everything unboosted. **Play Rough** is the anti-Fairy tech (Fairy is its *only* weakness, and Fairies wall its Dark/Ghost STABs), and **Detect** buys a safe turn to Mega-evolve or dodge a predicted priority hit. You Mega-evolve, get a clean turn from the support, and start slicing.
- **Rillaboom (Grassy Surge, Occa Berry).** The engine. **Grassy Surge** sets Grassy Terrain — healing the team 1/16 each turn, halving enemy Earthquakes, and powering **Grassy Glide** (Grass priority *while terrain is up*, a second priority answer for frail Absol's checks). **Wood Hammer** is the big hit, **High Horsepower** (95-BP Ground) covers Steel/Fire/Rock, **Fake Out** opens tempo, and the **Occa Berry** cushions a super-effective Fire hit so Rillaboom isn't instantly removed by the Fire attacks that punish Grass.
- **Milotic (Competitive, Grassy Seed).** The glue and the anti-Intimidate answer. **Competitive** turns every enemy stat-drop into a **+2 Sp. Atk** boost — so an opposing Intimidate or Icy Wind *feeds* Milotic. **Grassy Seed** gives **+1 Defense** the moment it lands in Rillaboom's terrain. **Scald / Icy Wind** (speed control + burns) with **Recover / Protect** make it a wall that also punishes. This is the **standard meta Milotic** (all four moves are top-usage).
- **Sneasler (Unburden, Focus Sash).** The speed-control breaker and the topic's **5th distinct Unburden trigger**. The **Focus Sash** guarantees it survives one hit; when the Sash breaks, **Unburden doubles its Speed**, and a 189-Speed Sneasler becomes untouchable. **Feint** is the tech pick — a +2-priority move that **removes the opponent's Protect**, cracking open a turtling foe so Absol or a partner lands the KO. **Close Combat / Dire Claw** (its signature 80-BP Poison with a status chance) are the STABs; **Fake Out** for tempo.
- **Gholdengo (Good as Gold, Choice Scarf).** The disruptor. **Good as Gold** makes it immune to status *moves* (Will-O-Wisp, Thunder Wave, Spore). **Choice Scarf + Trick** is the off-meta twist: fire **Make It Rain** (Champions: a **spread** Steel nuke that drops its own Sp. Atk 2 after use) / **Shadow Ball** / **Power Gem**, then **Trick** the Scarf onto a setup sweeper or a wall to lock it into one move. A Scarf'd Gholdengo also just outruns things and revenge-kills.
- **Salamence (Intimidate, Life Orb).** The pivot that ties it together — **not a Mega**. It brings **Intimidate** on entry (softening the physical attackers that could revenge Absol), sets **Tailwind** for team-wide speed, and provides the **Fire coverage this style usually lacks** via **Heat Wave** (spread Fire) plus **Draco Meteor** for a Life-Orb nuke. **Protect** for safety and Tailwind timing.

## Game plan

1. **Lead the shell, not the cannon.** Open with two of Rillaboom / Salamence / Milotic to set **Grassy Terrain + Tailwind + Intimidate** and Fake Out something. Keep Absol in the back until the board is safe.
2. **Clear Absol's answers first.** Its killers are priority users and Fairies. Intimidate + Icy Wind + Fake Out slow and soften them; **Feint** strips a Protect; **Trick** cripples a wall. Then bring Absol in on a free turn.
3. **Mega, then slice.** Mega-evolve (Detect turn if you need safety), and under Tailwind fire **Night Slash / Shadow Claw** (Sharpness) — **Play Rough** anything Fairy that thinks it walls you.
4. **Keep the terrain up.** Grassy Glide priority + the passive heal keep the frail core alive; re-set with Rillaboom if it lapses.
5. **Sneasler is your second win-con.** If Absol gets revenged, a Sash-broken **Unburden Sneasler at doubled Speed** cleans up — Feint through Protect, Close Combat / Dire Claw to finish.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **No commentary — this is a VOD read, not a coached teambuild.** The sets are exact (straight off the preview), but there's no creator explanation of *why*, and **no game was narrated**; the strategic reasoning here is inferred from the mechanics, not stated on stream. The five opponents shown (Mega Baxcalibur, Mega Banette, Mega Salamence, Trick Room, Mega Gardevoir) are from the video's own chapter list.
- **Tera types unknown.** The preview screen shows base types, not Teras — so no Tera is claimed here. In practice each of these has a Tera set; treat any Tera choice as your own.
- **Absol is genuinely fragile.** Sharpness makes it hit like a truck, but it dies to a single priority move (Grassy Glide, Aqua Jet, Extreme Speed, Sucker Punch) or any Fairy hit. It is a *finisher*, not a lead — respect that or you'll trade your Mega for a Focus-Sash chip.
- **Single Intimidate, single Mega.** Only Salamence carries Intimidate (no Incineroar), and Absol Z is the lone Mega — so you don't get the double-Intimidate cushion some frailer teams lean on, and you can't pivot to a second Mega. The Grassy-Terrain heal and Tailwind are what keep the glass core standing.
- **The Gholdengo is a disruptor, not a bulky pivot.** Choice-Scarf + Trick locks *it* into one move too — use Trick deliberately (onto a wall or setup sweeper), not as a panic button.

## Key Takeaways

- **Mega Absol Z is a hyperfast Sharpness glass cannon** — Dark/Ghost, base 154 Atk / 151 Spe (BST 565) with **no bulk gain** (65/60/60), **Sharpness ×1.5 on slicing moves** (Night Slash + Shadow Claw), immune to Normal/Fighting/Psychic and **weak only to Fairy** — which is exactly why it runs **Play Rough** for its own coverage. The whole team exists to hand this frail finisher a safe, fast turn.
- **The shell is a Grassy-Terrain + speed-control cocoon** — Rillaboom (Grassy Surge, Grassy Glide priority, **Occa Berry** Fire-cushion) + **Grassy-Seed Milotic** (+1 Def, Competitive) + **Life-Orb Salamence** (Intimidate + Tailwind + **Heat Wave** = the Fire coverage these Grass builds usually lack).
- **Focus-Sash Unburden Sneasler = the topic's 5th distinct Unburden trigger** — after Psychic Seed, White Herb, Swagger+Lum, and Grassy Seed. Sash breaks → Speed doubles → **Feint** strips Protect for the follow-up KO.
- **Choice-Scarf + Trick Gholdengo** is an off-meta disruptor (vs the usual Protect/Nasty Plot) — Good as Gold status immunity + a Scarf it can dump onto a wall via Trick.
