## Identity: six anime picks that secretly combo

The pitch is nostalgia — rebuild Ash's strongest anime team — but the payoff is that the six pieces **connect in unexpected ways**, and most of them dodge what the current meta is built to punish. The centerpiece is a **guaranteed-critical-hit engine**, wrapped in speed control, redirection, and a Protean sweeper.

## The engine: guaranteed critical hits

The team's win-condition is a **100%-crit Goodra**:

- **Noivern's Dragon Cheer** raises its allies' critical-hit ratio by **+1** — and by **+2 for a Dragon-type ally** like Goodra.
- **Goodra's Scope Lens** adds **+1** crit stage on its own.
- **+2 (Dragon Cheer) + +1 (Scope Lens) = +3 crit stages, which is a guaranteed (100%) critical hit** in Gen 9.

And a crit doesn't just deal 1.5× — **it ignores the target's Defense/Sp.Def boosts AND Reflect/Light Screen**. So after **one turn of Dragon Cheer**, Goodra's wide coverage (Draco Meteor / Muddy Water / Thunderbolt / Ice Beam) **crits straight through** setup sweepers and screen walls that would stonewall anyone else. As the creator puts it: one turn of setup is more than worth it.

## The other pieces (why they secretly fit)

- **Mega Greninja (Protean).** You can't have *Ash*-Greninja, so **Mega Greninja** stands in — and its **Protean** changes its type to whatever move it clicks (so every move is STAB). Because **Mega-evolving re-applies Protean**, it gets a "fresh" type-shift on the turn it Megas. **Grass Knot** (swappable to Ice Beam) is deliberately kept "for a certain someone" — the bulky Water/Grounds it otherwise can't crack.
- **Noivern (Telepathy) — the glue.** Beyond Dragon Cheer, **Telepathy makes Noivern immune to its own allies' spread moves**, so Greninja's **Surf** and Goodra's spread coverage can be fired freely with Noivern on the field. It's also the team's **second Tailwind setter** in an otherwise frail squad, with **Draco Meteor** as its lone attack (Focus Sash lets it survive to set up).
- **Pikachu (Light Ball + Lightning Rod).** People sleep on unevolved Pikachu — but **Light Ball doubles both its Attack and Sp. Atk**, so a full-Attack **Volt Tackle (120 BP)** hits genuinely hard, and **Fake Out + Helping Hand** support the setup. **Lightning Rod draws in Electric moves** (immunity + a boost) to cover the team.
- **Hawlucha (Unburden + White Herb) — the "poor man's Sneasler."** It clicks **Close Combat**, the **White Herb instantly undoes the Defense/Sp.Def drop**, and consuming the herb **triggers Unburden → double Speed**. Now itemless, its **Acrobatics doubles from 55 to 110 BP** — a fast Fighting/Flying two-STAB sweeper. **Upper Hand** (the creator's pick over Encore) punishes enemy priority.
- **Talonflame (Gale Wings + Sharp Beak).** **Gale Wings** gives its Flying moves **+1 priority at full HP**, so a **Sharp-Beak Brave Bird** revenge-kills the format's hot Fighting-types (Sneasler) before they move; it also brings **Tailwind** for the frail team.

## Game plan

1. **Set up the crit, or the speed.** Against bulky/setup teams, lead **Noivern + Goodra**, click **Dragon Cheer**, and start critting through everything. Against fast teams, lead **Talonflame** (Tailwind) or a Fake-Out Pikachu to control tempo.
2. **Mega Greninja is your flex win-con** — Protean coverage + Telepathy-safe Surf spam handles most boards; it's the safe lead into Intimidate/Fake-Out teams (it doesn't care about Intimidate and can one-shot with the right typing).
3. **Hawlucha closes** — once its White Herb pops, it's a doubled-Speed, 110-BP Acrobatics sweeper.
4. **Pikachu Lightning Rod** absorbs enemy Electric all game; keep it as a support pivot behind screens/Tailwind.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **It's a fragile team.** Several members (Noivern, Pikachu, Hawlucha) are frail; you lean on **Focus Sash / White Herb / Tailwind / Fake Out** to buy the one setup turn you need. Get read and focus-fired and it can crumble.
- **The crit plan needs Noivern alive for a turn.** If Dragon Cheer never goes off, Goodra is "just" a wide-coverage attacker — good, not game-ending. The creator notes Goodra in unskilled hands "isn't that big a threat."
- **Everything checked out.** Unusually, the adversarial verify pass found **no errors and nothing to overturn** — the crit-stack math (+3 = 100%, crits ignore boosts/screens), Protean-on-Mega, White-Herb→Unburden→Acrobatics-110, Gale Wings, Telepathy, and Light Ball (doubles *both* attacking stats) are all exactly as described. (Two precision notes only: Sharp Beak is *exactly* +20%, and Lightning Rod draws *all* Electric moves, spread included.)

## Key Takeaways

- **A guaranteed-crit engine is the core** — **Noivern Dragon Cheer (+2 crit for Dragons) + Goodra Scope Lens (+1) = 100% crits** that **ignore the enemy's Defense boosts and screens**. One setup turn and Goodra crits through walls.
- **The anime picks secretly combo** — **Telepathy** (dodge allied Surf/spread), **Lightning Rod** (redirect Electric), **Gale Wings** (priority revenge), and a **White Herb / Unburden / Acrobatics** Hawlucha all pull real weight.
- **Mega Greninja stands in for Ash-Greninja** — Protean makes every move STAB, and Mega-evolving refreshes it.
- **Light Ball unevolved Pikachu is not a joke** — doubled Attack *and* Sp. Atk make Volt Tackle hit hard, with Fake Out + Helping Hand support.
