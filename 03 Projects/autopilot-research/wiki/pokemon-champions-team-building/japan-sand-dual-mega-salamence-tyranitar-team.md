## Identity: an old archetype, re-pointed at the new meta

"Japan Sand" is a **2010s VGC classic** — **Tyranitar** sets sand with **Sand Stream**, **Excadrill** turns that sand into **doubled Speed** via **Sand Rush** and sweeps, and a **Mega** does the heavy breaking. metalderk revives it for Champions and grafts on a second layer to answer the day-one metagame: a **Psychic-Terrain sub-engine** built to beat the **Rillaboom + Sneasler priority spam** that everyone is running.

That second layer is the clever part. **Indeedee-Male's Psychic Surge** sets **Psychic Terrain**, which does three jobs at once:
1. **Turns off enemy priority** — grounded Pokémon can't be hit by Fake Out, Sucker Punch, Quick Attack, or **Grassy Glide**, neutering the Rillaboom/Sneasler game plan.
2. **Overrides the opponent's Grassy Terrain** — you reset their terrain with yours, so their Grassy Glide loses its priority entirely.
3. **Powers Expanding Force**, which in Psychic Terrain becomes a **boosted spread move** that **one-shots the opposing Sneasler** (Fighting/Poison takes Psychic at **4×**).

And sand and terrain are **different layers that stack** — so you're running **your sandstorm and your psychic terrain simultaneously**, giving the team *two* Speed engines (Sand-Rush Excadrill and a Psychic-Seed **Unburden** Sneasler) plus Salamence's Tailwind.

**It carries two Mega Stones** — **Salamencite and Tyranitarite** — and you bring **one Mega per game**: Mega Salamence as the Aerilate spread nuke most matchups, **Mega Tyranitar** as a bulkier sand-setter into psychic/Gardevoir cores (which is exactly the swap metalderk makes in game 3).

## The pieces

- **Tyranitar (Sand Stream, Tyranitarite).** The engine's foundation — sets the sand that powers Excadrill, and covers Steel/Poison/Ghost with **Rock Slide / Knock Off / Low Kick**. It's the **second Mega option**: metalderk notes Tyranitar is "so good it doesn't need a Mega," so most games you leave the Mega for Salamence — but into a Gardevoir/psychic core you **Mega-evolve Tyranitar** for the extra bulk (it keeps Sand Stream either way). **Knock Off** strips items; **Low Kick** nukes heavy targets.
- **Excadrill (Sand Rush, Life Orb).** The sweeper. In sand its **Speed doubles**, outrunning the format, and a Life Orb **Iron Head / Rock Slide / High Horsepower** off its huge Attack tears through the board. Iron Head is the Fairy/Rock/Ice answer; High Horsepower (95 BP Ground) is STAB into Steels and Fire.
- **Mega Salamence (Aerilate).** The primary Mega and spread threat: **Aerilate** turns **Hyper Voice into a Flying-type spread nuke** hitting both foes, backed by **Dragon Pulse** (special Dragon STAB) and **Tailwind** for team speed. It enters with **Intimidate** before evolving. *Caveat metalderk stresses:* Salamence is **Flying, so it's not grounded** — Psychic Terrain does **not** shield it from Fake Out or priority.
- **Indeedee-Male (Psychic Surge, Focus Sash).** The terrain setter and the anti-meta tech. **Psychic Surge** sets the terrain that blanks enemy priority and overrides Grassy Terrain; **Expanding Force** becomes a **boosted spread move in the terrain** that OHKOs the Fighting/Poison Sneasler (4×); **Mystical Fire** is the **anti-Archaludon** tech (special Fire that also drops the target's Sp. Atk); **Focus Sash** guarantees it lives to set up. The **male** is the offensive Indeedee (higher attacking stats) — deliberately chosen over the support female.
- **Sneasler (Unburden, Psychic Seed).** The second speed engine. In Psychic Terrain the **Psychic Seed** gives +1 Sp. Def and is **consumed → Unburden doubles Speed** ("Tailwind without setting Tailwind"). Then **Close Combat / Dire Claw** (its 80-BP Poison signature — **nerfed to a 30% status chance in Champions**, down from the main-series 50%) / **Rock Slide** clean up, with **Fake Out** for tempo. *(This is the topic's **Psychic-Seed** Unburden trigger — the same one [[pokemon-champions-team-building/mega-baxcalibur-terrain-team|Chill's Baxcalibur team]] uses.)*
- **Milotic (Competitive, Sitrus Berry).** The glue and the anti-Intimidate/anti-Incineroar wall. **Competitive** turns any enemy stat-drop (Intimidate, Icy Wind, Parting Shot) into **+2 Sp. Atk**, so it *wants* to be Intimidated; **Scald** burns, **Icy Wind** is backup speed control, and **Life Dew** heals the whole team 1/4 — metalderk repeatedly stalls games out with **Life Dew + Tailwind** while a partner attacks.

## Game plan

1. **Set the two layers.** Lead Tyranitar + Excadrill for the raw-speed sand opening, or lead Indeedee-M to get **Psychic Terrain** down when the enemy is a Rillaboom/Sneasler priority team — the terrain turns their whole game plan off.
2. **Pick your Mega by matchup.** Default to **Mega Salamence** (Aerilate Hyper Voice + Tailwind). Against psychic/Gardevoir cores, bring **Mega Tyranitar** instead for the bulk — you still get sand from Sand Stream.
3. **Sweep on doubled Speed.** In sand, **Excadrill** outruns and Iron Head / Rock Slide / High Horsepower the board; if you led terrain, a **Psychic-Seed Unburden Sneasler** is your second fast breaker.
4. **Expanding Force the priority users.** Under your terrain, Indeedee-M's spread Expanding Force blanks enemy Sneaslers and softens the field — and their Grassy Glide does nothing.
5. **Grind with Milotic when you can't sweep.** Life Dew + Tailwind + Scald burns stalls out walls; Competitive punishes any Intimidate switch-in.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Archaludon is the check — "brick Archaludon."** metalderk flags it as the team's most annoying answer (he faces two in one game); **Mystical Fire** (Indeedee) is the tech for it, but under enemy rain it doesn't hit hard, so it leans on the −Sp.Atk drop. Respect Archaludon rain.
- **Your own Psychic Terrain cuts both ways.** It stops enemy priority on grounded mons, but it *also* stops **your** Fake Out (terrain affects everyone), and it doesn't cover your **Flying** Salamence — which can still be Fake Out'd, Ice Sharded, or priority'd.
- **The Mega choice is a commitment.** Two stones, one Mega per game — bring the wrong one (Salamence into a hard psychic matchup, or Tyranitar when you needed the spread nuke) and you're down a win condition. metalderk misplays this once on stream (forgetting First Impression / a mega timing) — the team is powerful but demands matchup reads.
- **Borrowed sheet, and the history is loose.** metalderk is showcasing someone else's tournament-winning sheet (credit in his video) and nods to the 2010s "Japan Sand" lineage / a Sejun-Park-era reference — treat the history as flavor, not a citation. The sets are exact; EV spreads are the sheet's, not reproduced here.
- **Result is a 4-0 ladder snapshot, not a tournament run.** It beat a **Mega Golisopod** team, two **Archaludon rain** teams, and a **Mega Gardevoir psychic** team to hit Master Ball — a strong sign, but ladder Bo1, small sample.

## Key Takeaways

- **Japan Sand, modernized:** the classic **Sand Stream Tyranitar → Sand-Rush Excadrill → Mega breaker** core, grafted onto a **Psychic-Terrain sub-engine** built specifically to shut off the Rillaboom/Sneasler priority meta (terrain **blocks grounded priority**, **overrides Grassy Terrain**, and powers a spread **Expanding Force** that 4×-OHKOs enemy Sneasler).
- **Two Speed engines + Tailwind, and two Megas.** Sand-Rush Excadrill *and* Psychic-Seed **Unburden** Sneasler both hit doubled Speed; Salamence adds Tailwind. The team carries **Salamencite + Tyranitarite** and picks one Mega per matchup (Salamence spread nuke by default; **Mega Tyranitar** into psychic cores).
- **Sand and terrain stack** — different layers, both up at once; that coexistence is the whole trick.
- **Milotic is the anti-Intimidate glue** — Competitive turns enemy stat-drops into +2 Sp. Atk, Life Dew keeps the frail sand core healthy, Icy Wind is backup speed control.
- **Ground-truth teamsheet from a no-paste-trust source** — read off the on-screen team code after the creator warned his verbal pastes were wrong; 4-0 to Master Ball, including a **win over Mega Golisopod** (the very archetype that beat the earlier launch-week Mega teams).
