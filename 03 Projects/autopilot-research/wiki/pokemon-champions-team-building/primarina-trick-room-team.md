> **Source:** [Đội hình Primarina Trick Room này đã nhanh chóng đạt top 15 Rank Champions đầu mùa M-5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rXu-okYOad0) — `rXu-okYOad0`, **Duy Plays Poké** (Vietnamese), 2026-08-20, **38:38**. A review of a Trick Room team (originally by creator **Pok MD**) that reached **top 15** in the early Champions season M-5. The creator calls it "anti-meta" and easy to pilot.
> **Scope note:** the video **only details the 4 core Pokémon** — Primarina, Galarian Slowking, Incineroar, and Mega Scovillain — which he says handle most of the meta. The other 2 team slots aren't shown, so this guide documents the **4-Pokémon core** (validated + verified), not a full 6.

Mechanics adversarially fact-checked; **Mega Scovillain's existence confirmed** on Serebii's Champions Mega list.

## Identity: flip the speed order, then nuke

This is a **Trick Room** team built on slow, bulky Pokémon. **Galarian Slowking** sets Trick Room (for 5 turns the *slowest* Pokémon move first), and then **Primarina** — a slow Water/Fairy special attacker — gets to fire before everything. Because the whole core is slow (Slowking base **30**, Primarina & Incineroar **60**, Scovillain **75**), Trick Room turns their biggest weakness into an advantage.

What makes it "anti-meta" is the **pivot** engine: Slowking's **Chilly Reception** both **disrupts enemy weather** (it sets snow) *and* **switches Slowking out safely**, which — with **Regenerator** healing it 1/3 on the way out — lets you **re-set Trick Room again and again** instead of once per game. That same pivot is how the team **escapes trap/Perish-style teams** (Gengar + Incineroar): you just chill-reception out of the lock.

## How the core works

- **Primarina — the win-con.** **Liquid Voice** turns **Hyper Voice** into a Water-type spread attack *with STAB* — a big AoE nuke under Trick Room. **Moonblast** covers the Dragons that would otherwise wall it (Garchomp, Dragonite). **Calm Mind** snowballs its Special Attack/Defense so it becomes both nuke and wall. Built bulky, it survives to keep clicking.
- **Galarian Slowking — the engine.** **Trick Room** flips the speed order; **Chilly Reception** (its signature move) sets **snow** — overwriting enemy sun/rain to blunt Solar Beam / Electro — *and* pivots Slowking out; **Regenerator** heals it 1/3 as it leaves, so you can bring it back and **re-establish Trick Room repeatedly**. **Acid Spray** drops a target's Special Defense **−2**, teeing up a Primarina one-shot. **Sitrus Berry** makes it very hard to OHKO through the setup.
- **Incineroar — the protector.** **Intimidate** to blunt physical attackers, **Fake Out** to freeze a threat while Slowking sets Trick Room, and **Throat Chop** — which **silences sound-based moves for two turns**, shutting off Sylveon's Hyper Voice and Gengar's sound attacks. **Parting Shot** for a debuff-and-pivot.
- **Mega Scovillain — the harasser.** **Rage Powder** redirects the opponent's single-target attacks onto itself, protecting the setup. Its Champions Mega ability **Spicy Spray** is what the creator uses to **pressure/burn the opponent's physical attackers** (extra cover for your special core). **Giga Drain** deals damage while healing it back, and a Fire STAB (Flamethrower) rounds out coverage.

## Why it's "anti-meta"

- **Weather war:** **Chilly Reception** is a move (it resolves after switch-ins, Mega-evolution weather, and Prankster moves), so you can time it to **overwrite** the opponent's sun or rain — neutering Charizard-Y and rain cores.
- **Speed war:** **Trick Room** flips fast offense on its head; your bulky core then out-moves everything.
- **Trap teams:** **Chilly Reception + Regenerator** let Slowking **escape Perish/lock teams** (Gengar + Incineroar) that would otherwise pin you.
- **Sound spam:** **Throat Chop** silences the Hyper-Voice Sylveons and Gengars for two turns.

## Game plan (the creator's default, ~90% of games)

Lead **Slowking + Incineroar**: Incineroar **Fake Outs** a dangerous threat so **Slowking safely casts Trick Room**, with **Primarina + Scovillain** in the back. From there: **Acid Spray** to break Special Defense → **Primarina Hyper Voice** sweeps; **Chilly Reception** whenever you need to heal Slowking and re-set the room; **Rage Powder** (Scovillain) to soak attacks while you reposition. The creator notes Trick Room usually goes up cleanly because the core is so bulky, and Sitrus + Regenerator keep Slowking recycling it.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Only 4 of 6 shown.** The video details this 4-Pokémon core; the remaining 2 team slots aren't revealed, so this guide covers the documented core only.
- **Scovillain is the least-slow (75).** It benefits from Trick Room less than the 30/60 members — fine as a support piece, but not a pure TR-abuser.
- **No Trick Room = much weaker.** Like all TR teams, if you can't get the room up (or it's Taunted / the setter is removed), the slow core is exposed. The Fake-Out + bulk + Chilly-Reception-recycling all exist to make setup reliable, but it's still the dependency.
- **"Spicy Spray" specifics:** it's Mega Scovillain's Champions ability; the creator uses it to punish physical attackers, but the exact trigger isn't spelled out in the video — treat the "burn physical Pokémon" framing as his description.

## Key Takeaways

- **Galarian Slowking is the engine** — Trick Room to flip speed, **Chilly Reception** to disrupt weather + pivot, Regenerator to **re-set the room again and again**, Acid Spray (−2 SpD) to enable the KO.
- **Primarina is the payoff** — Liquid Voice makes Hyper Voice a STAB Water spread nuke; Calm Mind snowballs it; Moonblast handles Dragons.
- **Incineroar + Mega Scovillain protect the setup** — Fake Out / Intimidate / **Throat Chop** (silences sound moves) + **Rage Powder** redirection and **Spicy Spray** pressure on physical attackers.
- **It's genuinely anti-meta** — beats fast offense (TR), sun/rain (Chilly Reception weather steal), and trap teams (pivot escape).
- **Champions Mega spotlight:** Mega Scovillain (Grass/Fire, **Spicy Spray**) — another novel Champions Mega, joining Floette/Froslass/Staraptor/Raichu-Y/Venusaur in the topic.
