# Guide — Rain + Double Perish Song (the Shadow-Tag Perish trap)

> **Source:** [Đội hình Perish Song và Mưa này sẽ giúp bạn leo Master đầu mùa cực dễ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dgJ4rD1GarA) — `dgJ4rD1GarA`, **Duy Plays Poké** (Vietnamese), 2026-08-05, **39:08** (credits the build to **CybertronVGC**). A team that has placed **top 1–3** in recent tournaments and flips between two modes to climb ladder easily.
> **Validated in PokéSynergy** — all six resolved clean (Mega Gengar = **Shadow Tag**, Mega Swampert = **Swift Swim**, Politoed = **Drizzle** + Perish Song, Vivillon = **Compound Eyes**; only one filler item name went unrecognized). Mechanics adversarially fact-checked.
> **The flip side of a fun matchup:** this is the **"Perish trap"** the [Primarina Trick Room team](primarina-trick-room-team.html) is built to *escape* (via Chilly Reception) — read them together.

## Identity: two modes on one shell — rain, or a Perish trap

The team is built on a **rain core** (Politoed + Mega Swampert + Archaludon) that can pivot into a **Double Perish Song trap** (Mega Gengar + Politoed) depending on the matchup. **Politoed is the linchpin** — it both **sets the rain** (Drizzle) *and* carries **Perish Song**, so it's in almost every line-up.

**The Perish trap** is the star plan: **Mega Gengar's Shadow Tag** stops the opponent from switching out, and **Perish Song** starts a 3-turn faint countdown on everything that hears it. Because they can't switch to reset the count, the trapped Pokémon simply **faint** — you win by outlasting, not by out-damaging. **Vivillon's Sleep Powder** (near-100% accurate via Compound Eyes) puts a threat to sleep so it can't fight back while the count ticks, and running **two** Perish Song users (Gengar *and* Politoed) makes the plan reliable.

## How the two modes work

- **Perish-trap mode (Mega Gengar + Vivillon/Politoed).** **Shadow Tag** locks the opponent in; **Vivillon Sleep Powder** sleeps the scariest of the two active Pokémon; **Gengar Perish Songs**, and now the trapped, sleeping foe can neither switch nor act while the Perish count runs to 0. Gengar is built **bulky** (HP/SpD) so it survives a Charizard Weather Ball and lives long enough to see the count out; **double Perish** (Politoed also has it) means you can re-apply if needed. Gengar's own Shadow Ball / Sludge Bomb add direct pressure.
- **Rain-offense mode (Politoed + Mega Swampert + Archaludon).** **Politoed Drizzle** sets rain → **Mega Swampert Swift Swim** doubles its Speed (outrunning even Mega Aerodactyl) for a physical sweep, **Archaludon Electro Shot** fires with **no charge turn in rain** (a huge Electric nuke), rain-boosted Water moves hit harder, and Vivillon's **Hurricane is 100% accurate** in rain.

## The four line-ups (the creator's own breakdown)

1. **Mega Gengar + Vivillon** (his favorite): Sleep Powder + immediate Perish-trap; back with Politoed / Archaludon / Incineroar. Best when the opponent has nothing faster than Vivillon/Gengar.
2. **Incineroar + Mega Gengar**: the basic Perish trap — Fake Out lets Gengar Perish Song turn 1 (or pressure with Shadow Ball first, then Perish when they're down to two). Back with Politoed / Archaludon / Vivillon.
3. **Vivillon + Mega Swampert**: basic rain — use it when the opponent can contest weather (Charizard, Tyranitar). Back with Politoed / Archaludon.
4. **Politoed + Mega Swampert**: all-out rain — when the opponent can't fight for weather, set rain turn 1 and let Swampert tee off.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **Hypnosis on Politoed is a coin-flip** — the creator admits he "hasn't landed one" all season; treat it as a bonus, not a plan. (Vivillon's Compound-Eyes Sleep Powder is the reliable sleep.)
- **Shadow Tag doesn't trap everything.** **Ghost-types are immune to being trapped**, and pivots that leave *before* the count matters — **U-turn / Volt Switch / Chilly Reception** (see the Primarina TR team) — escape the Perish trap. Against those, lean on the rain mode instead.
- **Anti-Gengar leads** (Tyranitar, Garchomp) shut off the trap plan — the creator swaps to **pure rain** in those matchups.
- **Fire is weak in rain** — that's *why* Incineroar runs **Throat Chop over Flare Blitz** (rain halves Fire damage); Throat Chop also silences sound attackers.

## Key Takeaways

- **Shadow Tag + Perish Song is a trap you can't switch out of** — Mega Gengar locks the opponent in, Perish Song counts them down, and you win without KOs.
- **Politoed is the linchpin** — it sets the rain *and* carries the second Perish Song, so it's on almost every line-up.
- **Vivillon Compound Eyes** makes Sleep Powder ~98% — the reliable sleep that freezes a threat while Perish ticks.
- **One shell, two modes:** flip to **rain offense** (Swift Swim Swampert + no-charge Electro Shot Archaludon) when the Perish plan is bad.
- **It's beatable by pivots** — Ghost-types dodge Shadow Tag, and U-turn / Volt Switch / **Chilly Reception** escape the count (the Primarina TR team is built to do exactly this).
