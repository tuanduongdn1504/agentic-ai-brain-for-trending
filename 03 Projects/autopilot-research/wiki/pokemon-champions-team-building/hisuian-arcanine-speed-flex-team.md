## Identity: pick your speed control, every game

Built around **Hisuian Arcanine** (Rock/Fire) — a Focus-Sash pressure lead that **locks down Mega Charizard-Y** (Rock is 4× on Fire/Flying → recoil-free **Head Smash** OHKOs it). Around it sit **both** speed engines — **Tailwind** (Mega Staraptor) *and* **Trick Room** (Farigiraf) — plus a **priority backbone** (Fake Out, Sucker Punch, Extreme Speed). You choose your speed control to beat whatever the opponent brought.

## The three line-ups (the reviewer's own breakdown)

- **Line-up 1 — Mega Staraptor + Hisuian Arcanine** *(the default; vs Charizard / sun teams)*: Arcanine's **Focus Sash** guarantees immediate pressure with **Head Smash / Flare Blitz**; Staraptor sets **Tailwind**. Keep **Farigiraf** in the back to *pivot to Trick Room mid-game* if the speed war flips.
- **Line-up 2 — Mega Raichu + Kingambit** *(fast setup & nuke)*: Raichu's **Fake Out** buys **Kingambit a free Swords Dance** → **Sucker Punch** at +2 with **Life Orb** OHKOs most of the field.
- **Line-up 3 — double speed control** *(the scary one)*: run **Tailwind (Staraptor) *and* Trick Room (Farigiraf) in the same game** — so you can be fast when you want and slow-but-first when you want, beating almost any speed archetype the opponent has.

## Key tech (validated in-tool)

- **Mega Staraptor = Contrary:** Close Combat's Def/Sp.Def drops become **boosts** — it gets *bulkier* as it attacks. A rare "attack and tank" piece.
- **Hisuian Arcanine = Rock Head:** **Head Smash and Flare Blitz do zero recoil.** Head Smash (150 BP, 4× into Charizard-Y) is the anti-sun lock; Focus Sash means it always gets a hit off.
- **Farigiraf = Armor Tail:** blocks the opponent's priority (their Fake Out), so **your** Raichu/Arcanine move freely — and it's your Trick Room setter.
- **Kingambit = Supreme Overlord** (stronger per fainted ally) + priority **Sucker Punch** → the late-game cleaner behind Swords Dance.

## How to pick your line-up (this team's mode-selection)

| If the enemy… | Run… |
|---|---|
| has **Mega Charizard-Y / sun** | **Line-up 1** — lead Arcanine (Head Smash locks Charizard) + Staraptor Tailwind |
| is **fast offense / Tailwind** | **Trick Room** (Farigiraf) to flip them, or race with your own Tailwind |
| is **slow / bulky / Trick Room** | lean **Tailwind (Staraptor)** + the **Line-up 2** priority-nuke (Fake Out → SD Kingambit) |
| brings **their own speed control** | **Line-up 3** — set *both* Tailwind and Trick Room and out-flex them |
| leads **priority (Fake Out) spam** | **Farigiraf Armor Tail** shuts it off — you win the Fake-Out war |

## Meta connection

This is the **third dual-Mega, flexible-speed team** the topic has catalogued (after Wolfey's Steelix/Tyranitar and Zhang's Tyranitar/Froslass), and the **fourth pair of novel Champions Megas** (after Mega Floette / Mega Froslass, now **Mega Staraptor / Mega Raichu Y**). The pattern is emphatic: **the top of the Champions meta is "carry two Megas + multiple speed modes, and pick from the enemy team."** This team just pushes it furthest — *three* line-ups and a double-speed-control mode. It's now the **third preset** in the [Game Plan Assistant](steelix-synergy-guide.html) — the **"🔥 Arcanine · Speed-Flex"** toggle: paste an enemy team and it tells you which line-up to run (Arcanine-lead vs Charizard, Trick Room vs fast offense, or the double-speed Line-up 3) plus a matchup-risk flag.
