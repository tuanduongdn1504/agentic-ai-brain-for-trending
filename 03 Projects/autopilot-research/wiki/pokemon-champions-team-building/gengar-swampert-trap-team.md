## Identity: trap them in, then sleep them

The whole team runs on **Mega Gengar's Shadow Tag** — adjacent foes **can't switch out**. Where the [Perish Song team](rain-perish-song-team.html) uses that to run a faint-countdown, **this** team pairs it with **regular Swampert's Yawn**: Yawn makes a foe drowsy (asleep at the end of next turn) *unless it switches away* — and Shadow Tag means **it can't switch**, so the sleep lands. That frees Gengar to run **Substitute over Perish Song** and lean on its **offense** (Shadow Ball / Sludge Bomb threaten most of the format for super-effective damage).

The hook is **regular, non-Mega Swampert** — "a Pokémon that saw basically no usage in this format." It's bulky (survives a Life-Orb **Basculegion Wave Crash**), carries **Yawn** for the sleep combo, and **High Horsepower + Flip Turn** let it muscle the **sand** matchup (Tyranitar / Excadrill / Gholdengo) — the exact Pokémon that usually scare Gengar off the field. The rest is a flexible **offense-plus-speed-control** shell.

## The 6th slot: Swampert (Yawn trap) ↔ Kingambit (Perish Song)

The **core five are stable** (Gengar / Sneasler / Dragonite / Ninetales / Incineroar); the 6th flexes, and it changes Gengar's 4th move:

- **Swampert + Substitute Gengar** — the version in this video (Kelch's Victory Road win). Yawn + Shadow Tag guarantees sleep; Substitute dodges status and blocks *your own* Yawn from being wasted. The anti-sand pick.
- **Kingambit + Perish Song Gengar** — a version with **five of the same six** finished **top-4 in the biggest online tournament before Worlds**. Swap Substitute → **Perish Song** and you get the classic Shadow-Tag Perish trap as a second win condition (at the cost of Substitute's safety).

The importable list below is the **Swampert / Substitute** version (what this video builds and validated in PokéSynergy).

## How it plays — leads & combos (the creator's own breakdown)

The team is deliberately **flexible** — Cybertron stresses these are *examples*, not the only lines:

- **Gengar + Sneasler** — instant speed + pressure. Sneasler threatens Charizard / Mega Floette / Whimsicott super-effectively, so it's the go-to lead into **Whimsicott + Charizard + Floette** teams.
- **Gengar + Swampert** (the trap) — Mega-evolve Gengar and **Protect**, Swampert **Yawns** a scary threat; turn 2 pivot Gengar → Incineroar. The foe **can't switch** (Shadow Tag), so it falls asleep. Best when the enemy has a scary Mega you'd rather put to sleep than fight.
- **Gengar + Dragonite** — two **Fake-Out-immune** bodies (Gengar is airborne-ish via typing tricks; **Dragonite has Inner Focus**), so double-Fake-Out teams can't open on you. Big immediate damage + Dragonite bulk.
- **Swampert + Dragonite** — the anti-**setup / Trick-Room** lead. Those teams love to Fake Out — but Dragonite's **Inner Focus** ignores it, so they're forced to Fake Out the **Swampert** while **Dragonite Superpowers** the Incineroar / Kangaskhan (or Dragon Claws a Delphox / Blastoise). If they *don't* Fake Out, Swampert gets a **Yawn** off — disruptive either way.
- **Ninetales in the back** — the Garchomp answer: Mega-evolve Gengar + **Protect**, switch **Ninetales** in, **trap Garchomp** with Shadow Tag, then **Blizzard** it next turn for the KO (snow makes Blizzard 100%). **Encore** also shuts a foe into a useless move after turn one, and **Icy Wind + Dragonite** is a speed-drop → immediate-KO combo.

**The through-line:** *everything except Incineroar hits hard.* You win by finding the angle for super-effective damage and using **Shadow Tag to deny the switch** that would dodge it.

## Watch-outs (honest notes)

- **The sleep trap fails against Ghost-types.** Yawn + Shadow Tag only guarantees sleep on **non-Ghost** foes — **Ghosts are immune to Shadow Tag** and can freely switch out of the drowsy status. (A foe already behind a Substitute also blocks Yawn.) Against Ghosts, lean on offense/Perish instead.
- **Mega Gengar is frail and hard to pilot.** Even after Mega Evolution it isn't bulky — it survives a −1 (Intimidated) Life-Orb Garchomp Earthquake and not much more. It's flagged as "difficult to pilot"; the Intimidate glue (Incineroar) is what keeps it alive.
- **Sneasler has no Fake Out.** By choice — it runs **Dire Claw / Close Combat / Rock Slide / Protect** for pure offense, so this team's Fake-Out access is Incineroar only, and dedicated **Perish** end-games are harder than on a purpose-built Perish team.
- **Choice Scarf Ninetales is a surprise, but awkward to lead.** Its coverage outside Dragons is thin, so leading it alone invites a free Protect/switch; the creator prefers to *find a spot for it* mid-game rather than open with it.
- **Verifier refinements (independently checked, per the vault's wiki-verify rule):** **Dire Claw** is a **50% chance to inflict *one* of poison / paralysis / sleep** (~16.7% each), not a flat 30%; **High Horsepower is 85 BP** in Gen 9 (a nerf from older gens); and the Yawn+Shadow-Tag "guaranteed sleep" claim carries the **non-Ghost caveat** above. All three are folded into the text — none changed a team member.

## Key Takeaways

- **Shadow Tag + Yawn = a sleep you can't switch out of** — Mega Gengar traps, regular Swampert Yawns, and the drowsy foe can't escape (**non-Ghost only**). It's the [Perish trap](rain-perish-song-team.html)'s cousin, built around **Substitute + offense** instead of Perish Song.
- **Regular Swampert is the "new partner"** — bulky enough to eat a Life-Orb Basculegion Wave Crash, and its High Horsepower / Flip Turn punish the **sand** cores that normally chase Gengar away.
- **Dragonite runs Inner Focus, not Multiscale** — trading full-HP damage reduction for **Fake-Out immunity**, which is what makes the Swampert + Dragonite lead so strong into Fake-Out-reliant setup / Trick-Room teams.
- **Alolan Ninetales (Choice Scarf) is the speed toolbox** — snow makes **Blizzard 100%**, **Freeze-Dry** nukes Water/Ground threats, **Icy Wind** enables a Dragonite clean-up, and **Encore** shuts a foe down after a Gengar Protect.
- **Flex 6th** — Swampert + Substitute-Gengar (this video, Victory Road win) or **Kingambit + Perish-Song-Gengar** (top-4 the biggest pre-Worlds online tournament). Core five stay put.
