## What this is

Not a tutorial and not a tool — a **career-path opinion piece** from a working VN engineer who explicitly likes the technology (LLMs, vector DBs, embeddings, MCP) but rejects the hype-push. His answer is deliberately unglamorous: you don't need to be a frontier researcher; you need **fundamentals + the surrounding stack + ordinary engineering + a habit of building hard, real things.** The corpus' **2nd VN-practitioner career piece** (sibling to [[hoidanit-fullstack-vibe-coding/_index]]) and a practitioner counterpart to the book-grounded [[ai-engineering/_index]].
