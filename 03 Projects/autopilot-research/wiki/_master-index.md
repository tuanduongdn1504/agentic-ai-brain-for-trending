## adaptive-engineering-beyond-harness

**Rajiv Chandegra's AI Engineer *Europe* 2026 talk "Beyond the Harness: A Journey Towards Adaptative Engineering" — the corpus' FIRST source arguing AGAINST the fixed-harness paradigm the 32-article [[harness-engineering/_index]] flagship is built on.** Operator-submitted video [`qdZzND79mcg`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdZzND79mcg) (AI Engineer channel `@aiDotEngineer`, uploaded 2026-07-07 — **delayed publication of an April Europe-event talk, NOT World's Fair**; 37:01, ~6.4K views). Thesis: today's AI engineering steers agents with a **fixed harness** (vendor system prompt + `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md` + tools + roles + sequencing + loops) decided **before** runtime — reliable/auditable/traceable, **"Taylorism for AI"**, and the *right* answer for **complicated** problems. But as models improve exponentially and AI leaves the screen for the **messy real world** (multi-agent, multi-human, multi-institutional, physical = **complex**, not merely complicated), fixed harnesses turn brittle.
