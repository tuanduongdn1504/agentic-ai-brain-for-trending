# Alpha, Decay, Taste & the Half-Life of a Signature

> **Source:** Addy Osmani keynote [`n97BCfyFIvw`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n97BCfyFIvw), AI Engineer World's Fair 2026. The "career math" portion of the talk.

## Alpha and decay

- **Alpha** = the gap between what *you* can do today and what *current models* can do. That gap is real and valuable.
- **Decay** = the clock on that gap. If the thing that makes you special is a *capability*, the frontier will eventually come for it.

**The decay test** (run any skill through it):
- **Speed** — decayed.
- **Recall** — decayed (harnesses have memory).
- **Verification** — moving into harnesses, evals, static checks, model critique.
- **Taste** — decays *much more slowly*, but still resets as models learn from examples and preferences.
- **Judgment** — "a slope rather than a wall."

**Strategy:** don't cling to any one capability. **Keep moving your edge up a level.** This is *why* "what can the agent do?" is the wrong strategic question — the list of things it can't do keeps shrinking.

## Taste — the slow-decaying edge

Two quotes Osmani leans on:

- **Paul Graham** (confirmed, X, Feb 2026): *"When anyone can make anything, the big differentiator is what you choose to make."* Choosing what to make becomes the scarce skill.
- **Mitchell Hashimoto's** definition (the precise version): taste = *"the ability to **consistently** make high-quality qualitative judgments where no objective metric exists."* This puts taste **before the benchmark and before the market has voted.**

Osmani's caveats about taste:
- Taste can become "a magic word for whatever part of the work we don't want to explain yet" — resist that.
- Taste is **alpha too, not an eternal moat.** The best version of taste "is not mystique — it's making better calls and **leaving behind examples that your team and the system can learn from.**" (Turn taste into critique + examples → durable, teachable, and it improves the factory.)

## Edge vs signature — the actual career math

> "The half-life of an **edge** might be one model release. Speed, recall, verification, even taste all move as the frontier moves. But the half-life of a **signature** — your credibility, your expertise, the name on the work — is much longer."

- **Signature** = the person / team / institution that stands behind what shipped.
- **Skills earn leverage; accountability turns leverage into trust.**

This is the bridge to the answerability thesis: capabilities are rented from the frontier and decay; **accountability compounds** and is the thing worth building a career on. See [[engineer-of-the-future/osmani-answerability-thesis]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Alpha** (your edge over models) is real but subject to **decay** (the frontier catches up).
- Speed and recall already decayed; verification is being absorbed by harnesses; **taste decays slowly but still resets.**
- **Taste** = *consistently* making high-quality qualitative judgments where no metric exists yet (Hashimoto) — and it's most valuable when turned into teachable examples/critique.
- **Half-life of an edge ≈ one model release; half-life of a signature is far longer.** Skills earn leverage; **accountability turns leverage into trust.**
- Career strategy: keep moving your edge up a level; invest in the signature, not any single capability.
