## 2. Top-5 fixes (drill these; fixing #1–#3 alone changes everything)

1. 🔴 **Scrum Master** `/ˈskrʌm ˈmæstər/` — Vietnamese has no `/skr/` cluster, so it reduces to "drum." **Build it:** say **S–K–R** separately, then blend → **"SKRUM"** (rhymes with *strut*) → **"SKRUM MASS-ter"** (curl the tongue for the final -r). Minimal pair: **scrum vs drum** — pop the `/sk/`. Drill ×5.
2. 🔴 **helped** `/hɛlpt/` — three mouth positions: tongue-to-roof (`l`) → lips pop (`p`) → tongue-to-teeth (`t`) = **"HEL-P-T"**. Drill: help→helped, hope→hoped, jump→jumped.
3. 🟠 **Final `-s` plurals — the rule, not the word.** English *always* marks plural audibly: *types* `/taɪps/`, *years* `/jɪrz/`, *teams* `/timz/`, *skills* `/skɪlz/`, *methodologies* `/-dʒiz/`. Dropping it sounds beginner. Read your lines exaggerating the ending: "three **years‑z**", "different **types‑s** of **teams‑z**", "my **skills‑z**".
4. 🟠 **agile** `/ˈædʒəl/` — stress **syllable 1**: **AG-ile**, not a-GILE. Drill: agile / fragile / mobile.
5. 🟠 **Past `-ed` — the rule.** Three sounds: `/t/` (**helped**), `/d/` (**earned** `/ɝnd/`), `/ɪd/` (**shifted** `/ˈʃɪftɪd/`, **transitioned**). Feel the tiny ending: "I **transitioned**… my interest **shifted**… I **earned**…".

**Bonus words:** **Kanban** = "KAN-ban" (both short-a, stress 1) · **XP** = "EKS-PEE" (audible `ks`) · **enthusiasm** = en-**THOO**-zee-azm (`th` = tongue at teeth, voiceless) · **Thank you** = link the `k` into "you": "than**k-y**ou".

**Same three sounds Vietnamese lacks show up all over the NCC script too:** `/θ/` (think, both, strength), `/skr/` (Scrum), `/-lpt/` and other end-clusters. Drilling them here upgrades every English answer.
