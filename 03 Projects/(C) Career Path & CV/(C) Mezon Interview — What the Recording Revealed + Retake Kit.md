## 2. The free intelligibility self-test loop *(do this daily — it's an objective meter)*

You already have the perfect tool on your phone.

1. Open **iPhone Dictation** (the mic on the keyboard in Notes/Messages) OR Apple Translate set to **English only** (not conversation mode).
2. Read **one answer below, slowly**, one sentence at a time.
3. **Look at what it typed.** If you can read it back and it's ~80% correct → that sentence is intelligible. If it's gibberish → that sentence failed.
4. **Re-say the failed sentence slower**, over-articulating final consonants, until it transcribes cleanly.
5. Repeat until a whole answer transcribes readably.

This is the same machine-listener test that caught "Scrum Master → drum matter." Now use it as **training**, not judgment. **Goal: every answer below transcribes at ~80%+ before you retake.**

Focus while doing it (from your accent sheet):
- **Land final consonants:** reac**T**, buil**T**, tes**T**, fixe**D**, app**S**, year**S**.
- **Slow down** — pause between sentences, not inside them.
- **Short sentences** — one idea per sentence. ASR (and interviewers) catch short clear sentences far better than long ones.

## 3. Interview mechanics — fix these before the retake

- ❌ **No translation app running.** It fights for the mic and you don't need it. The questions are simple (they're all below).
- ✅ **Quiet room**, phone/mic **close to your mouth**.
- ✅ **Speak ~30% slower** than feels natural. Slow + clear always beats fast.
- ✅ **Start within 2 seconds** (silence auto-advances). Use a bridge: *"Sure, let me explain."*
- ✅ **Keep answers 20–35 seconds.** Short. Then stop.
