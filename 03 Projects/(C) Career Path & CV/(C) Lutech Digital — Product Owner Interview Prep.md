## §1 — Core Product Owner knowledge *(nail the fundamentals)*

- **What a PO is accountable for:** *maximizing the value of the product* from the team's work. You **own the Product Backlog** — develop and communicate the **Product Goal**, create and **order** backlog items, ensure they're transparent and understood. One accountable person, not a committee (you can delegate, but you're accountable).
- **PO vs Scrum Master vs BA (they'll test this since you come from SM):**
  - **PO** = *what to build and why* — value, priorities, backlog ownership.
  - **Scrum Master** = *how the team works* — process, facilitation, servant-leadership.
  - **BA** = requirements analysis/documentation — bridges business & tech, often *doesn't own* priority.
  - Say it crisply: *"The Scrum Master optimizes the team; the Product Owner optimizes the product."*
- **Vision → roadmap → backlog:** vision (the north star) → **roadmap** (outcome-based, e.g. Now / Next / Later — themes and outcomes, not a dated feature list) → **backlog** (near-term, ordered items).
- **Prioritization frameworks — know 2-3 well:**
  - **Value vs Effort** (2×2 — quick wins first).
  - **RICE** = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort.
  - **MoSCoW** = Must / Should / Could / Won't.
  - (Bonus: **Kano** — basic/performance/delighter; **WSJF** — cost-of-delay ÷ job-size, SAFe.)
- **User stories:** *"As a [user], I want [goal], so that [benefit]."* Good stories follow **INVEST** (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable).
- **Acceptance criteria:** the conditions that make a story "done/correct" — **Given / When / Then** (Gherkin) or a checklist. Definition of Ready (before pulling in) vs Definition of Done (quality bar).
- **MVP & releases:** MVP = smallest version that delivers value and lets you *learn*. Iterative rollouts, **feature flags**, staged/percentage rollouts.
- **A/B testing:** run two variants, measure one primary metric, need enough sample for **statistical significance**; decide by data, not opinion. *(You've shipped apps — you can speak to the technical side of A/B + feature flags credibly.)*

## §2 — Mobile-app product metrics *(they named these — LEARN them cold)*

- **Retention** — % of users who come back: **D1 / D7 / D30** retention. The #1 health metric for a mobile app.
- **DAU / MAU** — daily / monthly active users; **stickiness = DAU ÷ MAU**.
- **Conversion** — % completing a step in the **funnel** (install → signup → activation → purchase).
- **ARPU** — average revenue per user; **ARPPU** — per *paying* user.
- **LTV** — lifetime value (total revenue expected per user); **CAC** — cost to acquire a user; healthy when **LTV : CAC ≥ ~3:1**.
- **Churn** — % who stop using/paying.
- **Cohort analysis** — track a group of users over time (e.g. retention by install week).
- **How to use them (the PO skill):** *"I'd anchor the roadmap to a target metric — e.g. if D7 retention is weak, I prioritize onboarding and habit features, then A/B test and read the cohort."* That sentence shows you think like a PO, not just list definitions.

## §4 — Scenario & behavioral questions *(anchor to real experience + honesty)*

- **"Two stakeholders want opposite features — how do you decide?"** → *"Back to data and the product goal: which serves more users / more business value? I'd use RICE or value-vs-effort, make the trade-off transparent, and say no to one with a clear reason. A PO's job includes saying no."*
- **"How do you build a roadmap under uncertainty?"** → outcome-based Now/Next/Later, tied to metrics, revisited each cycle; avoid over-committing dates.
- **"How do you write a good user story / acceptance criteria?"** → INVEST + Given/When/Then; small, testable, valuable.
- **"Engineering says a feature is too big — what do you do?"** → *"Here my technical background helps — I can actually discuss the effort, slice the story smaller, or find a leaner way to test the value first."* (Your differentiator.)
- **"How do you prioritize the backlog?"** → value vs effort / RICE + the product goal + the metric you're moving.
- **"A launched feature isn't moving the metric — what now?"** → inspect the funnel/cohort, form a hypothesis, A/B test a change, or cut it. Data-driven, unattached to your own ideas.
- **Your real proof points:** owning the mobile client + release for **two shipped apps** (product feel + feasibility); **backlog prioritization with the PO** at Enouvo; **cross-functional coordination** as Scrum Master (Engineering/Design); mentoring/L&D (stakeholder communication).
