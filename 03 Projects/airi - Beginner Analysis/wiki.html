01 What it actually is

Domain: AI companionship / entertainment / VTuber — off both goals. AIRI gives a person a persistent, embodied, conversational AI character they own and self-host: not a livestream you watch (Neuro-sama), not a walled product (Character.AI), but a digital being that talks, emotes through an avatar, remembers, and plays games alongside you.

What makes it a worthwhile subject anyway is its architecture — a large, mature (~42.8k★), production-grade agentic system whose engineering touches four live goal threads. The domain is off-goal; the architecture is the prize. This is the exact shape of meetily v196 and OpenMontage v188.

02 Architecture — Brain / Ears / Mouth / Body

🧠

Brain

LLM reasoning via xsAI; game-playing agents (Minecraft · Factorio WIP · KSP planned); Telegram/Discord; in-browser DB (DuckDB-WASM + pglite); Memory Alaya (WIP); WebGPU local inference.

👂

Ears

Browser + Discord audio input; client-side speech recognition; voice-activity detection — fronted by unspeech, a universal ASR/TTS proxy.

👄

Mouth

Multi-provider TTS: ElevenLabs · Azure · OpenAI-compatible · Alibaba Cloud + local Kokoro TTS.

🧍

Body

VRM (3D) + Live2D (2D) avatars via Three.js; auto-blink, auto-lookaround, idle eye movement.

Delivered as stage-web (browser) · stage-tamagotchi (Electron desktop, native CUDA/Metal) · stage-pocket (mobile PWA). Web-first: WebGPU/WASM/Workers/WebSocket + HuggingFace Candle.

⭐ The on-goal prize: xsAI — a provider-agnostic LLM seam

A standalone TS SDK abstracting 30+ LLM providers (OpenAI · Anthropic Claude · Gemini · DeepSeek · Qwen · Ollama · vLLM · OpenRouter · many Chinese providers …) behind one interface. This is the vendor-seam blueprint — meetily v196's generate_summary(), the mosh-ai A2 seam, hireui's future first-LLM-feature seam.
